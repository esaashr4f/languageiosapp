## Before you start

This review covers all of part 4: the stems (lesson 4.1), qatal and yiqtol (4.2, 4.3), the wayyiqtol and weqatal chains (4.4, 4.5) and what the forms mean (4.6), the imperative, jussive and cohortative (4.7, 4.8), the participle and infinitive (4.9, 4.10), the weak verbs (4.11, 4.13 to 4.18), object suffixes (4.19), and the derived stems with their weak forms (4.20 to 4.24). The midway review (4.12) covered the first half; this one leans on the second.

Do each exercise before you read the answer. Each answer names the lesson to reread.

## Passage

The passage is attested: Genesis 22:9–11 and 13, word for word from the Leningrad Codex. Abraham reaches the mountain, builds the altar, and is stopped by the angel. Verse 12 was read in lessons 4.2 and 4.8. Lesson 8.4 reads verses 1 to 8.

## Exercises

1. Translate line 1. Why is the second verb, *ʾāmar*, a qatal?
2. Translate line 2. Give the root and class of *wayyaʿărōḵ* and *wayyāśem*.
3. Translate line 3. Explain *wayyiqqaḥ* and *lišḥōṭ*.
4. Translate line 4. Why is the angel's call a wayyiqtol, when it interrupts?
5. Translate line 5. Identify the stem of *neʾĕḥaz* and explain its first vowel.
6. Translate line 6. Take apart *wayyaʿălēhû*: stem, root, person, suffix.
7. List the wayyiqtols in lines 1–2 whose roots are weak, and name each class.
8. Rewrite line 3 as instructions to Abraham: "Stretch out your hand and take the knife", with an imperative and a weqatal.
9. Build "Abraham will build an altar there" (yiqtol).
10. Build "Let him build an altar" (jussive of *bānâ*).
11. Build "He was bound on the altar" with the niphal of *ʿāqaḏ*, as a constructed qatal.
12. Build "I will offer it as a burnt offering" (hiphil yiqtol of *ʿālâ*, 1SG + "it").
13. Build "When Abraham saw the ram, he took it", with *wayhî* and *kə-* + infinitive.
14. Build "Abraham was sitting under the tree" with a participle.
15. Find and fix the error: *\*wayyaʿăleh ʾeṯ-hāʾayil* for "and he offered the ram".
16. Find and fix the error: *\*wayyāḇōʾ ʾeṯ-hāʿēṣîm* for "and he brought the wood".
17. Find and fix the error: *\*ʾal šəlaḥ yāḏəḵā* for "do not stretch out your hand".
18. Find and fix the error: *\*hiṯšammēr* for "take care".
19. Find and fix the error: *\*wayyāšeḇ ʾaḇrāhām ʾel-nəʿārāyw* for "Abraham returned to his young men".
20. Find and fix the error: *\*yišmōr ʾōṯô* for "he will guard him", using a suffix on the verb.
21. Explain why *hinnēnî* has the suffix *-nî*, not *-î*.
22. What are the four views of the verb in lesson 4.6, and which one best explains why line 5's *wəhinnēh-ʾayil* is not a wayyiqtol?

## Worked answers

1. "They came to the place God had told him of, and Abraham built the altar there." *ʾāmar* is in a relative clause describing an earlier event, so qatal, "had told" (lessons 4.2, 6.2). The main events are wayyiqtols (lesson 4.4).
2. "He arranged the wood, bound his son Isaac, and laid him on the altar on top of the wood." *Wayyaʿărōḵ*: *ʿ-r-k*, first guttural (lesson 4.11). *Wayyāśem*: *ś-y-m*, hollow, the *î* shortened (lesson 4.15).
3. "Abraham reached out his hand and took the knife to slaughter his son." *Wayyiqqaḥ*: *l-q-ḥ*, with *l* treated like a first *n* (lesson 4.17). *Lišḥōṭ*: *lə-* + infinitive of *šāḥaṭ*, purpose (lesson 4.10).
4. "But the angel of the LORD called to him from heaven and said, 'Abraham, Abraham!' He said, 'Here I am.'" It is the next event in the story, so a wayyiqtol; the contrast is left to the English "but" (lesson 4.4; Gesenius §111e).
5. "Abraham looked up and saw: there was a ram behind him, caught in the thicket by its horns." *Neʾĕḥaz*: niphal qatal of *ʾ-ḥ-z*; the first guttural takes *ĕ*, and the prefix *ni-* becomes *ne-* (lessons 4.11, 4.20).
6. "Abraham went, took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son." *Way-yaʿălē-hû*: hiphil wayyiqtol 3SG.M of *ʿ-l-h* with the suffix *-hû* "him, it" (lessons 4.16, 4.19, 4.24).
7. *wayyāḇōʾû* (*b-w-ʾ*, hollow); *wayyiḇen* (*b-n-h*, third *h*); *wayyāśem* (*ś-y-m*, hollow). *Wayyaʿărōḵ* and *wayyaʿăqōḏ* are first-guttural only.
8. *šəlaḥ ʾeṯ-yāḏəḵā wəlāqaḥtā ʾeṯ-hammaʾăḵeleṯ*. Imperative, then weqatal continuing the command (lessons 4.5, 4.7).
9. *yiḇneh ʾaḇrāhām šām mizbēaḥ*. Third-*h* yiqtol in *-eh* (lesson 4.16).
10. *yiḇen mizbēaḥ*. The short jussive of *yiḇneh* (lessons 4.8, 4.16).
11. *neʿĕqaḏ ʿal-hammizbēaḥ*. Niphal qatal with a first guttural: *ne-* and *ĕ* (lesson 4.20; constructed, by pattern).
12. *ʾaʿălennû ləʿōlâ*. Hiphil yiqtol 1SG of *ʿālâ* with energic *-ennû* "it" (lessons 4.19, 4.24; by pattern).
13. *wayhî kirʾôṯ ʾaḇrāhām ʾeṯ-hāʾayil wayyiqqāḥēhû*. *Wayhî* + *kə-* + infinitive *rəʾôṯ*, then the main event with a suffix (lessons 4.10, 4.18, 4.19).
14. *ʾaḇrāhām yōšēḇ taḥaṯ hāʿēṣ*, or in a story *wəʾaḇrāhām yōšēḇ ...*. Participle as predicate; the time comes from the context (lesson 4.9).
15. *wayyaʿal ʾeṯ-hāʾayil* (hiphil, short form; Genesis 8:20 *wayyaʿal ʿōlōṯ*). The narrative uses the short form; *wayyaʿăleh* is the long form (lessons 4.16, 4.24).
16. *wayyāḇēʾ ʾeṯ-hāʿēṣîm*. "Bring" is the hiphil, with *ē*; *wayyāḇōʾ* is the qal "he came" (lesson 4.24).
17. *ʾal-tišlaḥ yāḏəḵā* (Genesis 22:12). Negative commands use *ʾal* + jussive, never the imperative (lessons 4.7, 4.8).
18. *hištammēr*. The *t* changes places with the sibilant (lesson 4.23; Gesenius §54b).
19. *wayyāšoḇ ʾaḇrāhām ʾel-nəʿārāyw* (Genesis 22:19). "Return" is the qal of hollow *š-w-b*; *wayyāšeḇ* would be the hiphil "brought back" (lessons 4.15, 4.24).
20. *yišmərēhû* or *yišmərennû*. Yiqtol + *-ēhû*, or with energic *-ennû*; the *ō* reduces before the suffix (lesson 4.19; by pattern).
21. *Hinnēh* is not a noun, and the "me" here is the object-type suffix *-nî*, as on verbs: "behold me" (lessons 4.19, 5.10).
22. Tense with converting waw, aspect, discourse function, and history (lesson 4.6). The discourse view explains it best: *wəhinnēh* + noun presents what Abraham sees, a background scene set off from the main line, which goes on with *wayyēleḵ* (lessons 4.6, 5.10).
