## Before you start

This review covers the first half of part 4: the verb template (lesson 4-1); thematic suffixes and the present stem (4-2); the object markers *m-*, *g-*, *gv-* (4-3) and who gets the plural *-t* (4-4); the imperfect (4-5); preverbs and the future (4-6); the conditional (4-7); series and screeves (4-8); the four classes (4-9); class 2 verbs in the present and future (4-10); going and coming (4-11); and the aorist of transitive verbs with the ergative (4-12). It also needs parts 2 and 3: nominative, dative, postpositions, possessives and the rest.

One rule runs through everything: first decide the class and the series, then the cases follow. Series I (present, imperfect, future, conditional): subject nominative, object dative. Series II (the aorist, so far): a class 1 subject ergative, its object nominative.

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread.

## Passage

**Summer at Grandmother's.** Constructed, using only words and grammar from parts 1 to 4 up to lesson 4-12. Mkhedruli first, then the transliteration.

1. შარშან ზაფხულში ბებიასთან ვცხოვრობდით, სოფელში.
   *šaršan zapxulši bebiastan vcxovrobdit, sopelši.*
2. ბებია დილას ადრე გვაღვიძებდა ხოლმე.
   *bebia dilas adre gvaɣviʒebda xolme.*
3. ერთხელ ბებიამ დიდი ნამცხვარი გამოაცხო და ყველა მეზობელი დაპატიჟა.
   *ertxel bebiam didi namcxvari gamoacxo da q̇vela mezobeli daṗaṭiža.*
4. საღამოს მეზობლები ეზოში შემოდიოდნენ, ბავშვები თამაშობდნენ და მღეროდნენ.
   *saɣamos mezoblebi ezoši šemodiodnen, bavšvebi tamašobdnen da mɣerodnen.*
5. ბაბუამ ბაღში ვაზი დარგო, მე კი ღობე შევღებე.
   *babuam baɣši vazi dargo, me ḳi ɣobe ševɣebe.*
6. ახლა ქალაქში ვცხოვრობთ, მაგრამ ზაფხულში ისევ სოფელში წავალთ.
   *axla kalakši vcxovrobt, magram zapxulši isev sopelši c̣avalt.*
7. ბებია ალბათ ისევ ადრე გაგვაღვიძებს!
   *bebia albat isev adre gagvaɣviʒebs!*
8. მე იქ დიდხანს დავრჩებოდი, მაგრამ სკოლა მალე იწყება.
   *me ik didxans davrčebodi, magram sḳola male ic̣q̇eba.*
9. — ნინო, სად მიდიხარ? — მაღაზიაში მივდივარ, პურს ვიყიდი.
   *— nino, sad midixar? — maɣaziaši mivdivar, ṗurs viq̇idi.*

## Exercises

1. Translate sentences 1 and 2.
2. Translate sentences 3 and 4.
3. Translate sentences 5 and 6.
4. Translate sentences 7 to 9.
5. In sentence 1, name the screeve of *vcxovrobdit* and take it apart piece by piece.
6. In sentence 2, take apart *gvaɣviʒebda*. Why is there no *-t* although "us" is plural?
7. In sentence 3, why is it *bebiam* and not *bebia*, and why *namcxvari* and not *namcxvars*?
8. In sentence 3, what tells you that *gamoacxo* is third person singular? What would "I baked" be?
9. In sentence 4, why is *mezoblebi* nominative, when sentence 3 had the ergative?
10. In sentence 5, explain *ševɣebe*: preverb, person, ending.
11. In sentence 6, what kind of future is *c̣avalt*, and why does it not have a new preverb?
12. In sentence 7, take apart *gagvaɣviʒebs* and name its screeve.
13. In sentence 8, name the screeve of *davrčebodi* and say how it is built. Which class is the verb?
14. In sentence 9, why *midixar* and not *modixar*? What would change if Nino were walking towards the speaker?
15. Say "Grandmother would bake a cake, but there is no sugar."
16. Say "I see you (one person), but you don't see me."
17. Say "The children were drawing in the yard, and Father was building a fence." Then put the second clause into the aorist.
18. Say "The new school will be built next year."
19. Say "We wrote the letter yesterday, and tomorrow we will send it."
20. Correct *\*Bebiam dilas adre gvaɣviʒebda.*
21. Correct *\*Babua vazs dargo.*
22. Correct *\*Vgxedav* (meaning "I see you").
23. Correct *\*Ninom maɣaziaši c̣avida.*
24. Correct *\*Bavšvebi izrdeben* and *\*Mat c̣erilebi dac̣eren* (meaning "they wrote the letters").

## Worked answers

1. "Last summer we lived at Grandmother's, in the village." *Bebia-s-tan* "at Grandmother's" (lesson 3-2). "Grandmother used to wake us early in the morning." The imperfect and *xolme* make it a habit (lesson 4-5).
2. "Once Grandmother baked a big cake and invited all the neighbours." Two aorists of class 1 verbs (lesson 4-12). "In the evening the neighbours would come into the yard, and the children played and sang." Three imperfects for the background (lesson 4-5).
3. "Grandfather planted a vine in the garden, and I painted the fence." "Now we live in town, but in summer we'll go to the village again."
4. "Grandmother will probably wake us early again!" "I would stay there a long time, but school begins soon." "'Nino, where are you going?' 'I'm going to the shop; I'll buy bread.'"
5. Imperfect. *v-cxovr-ob-di-t*: *v-* first person, root *cxovr*, thematic suffix *-ob*, imperfect *-di*, plural *-t*: "we lived, were living" (lessons 4-2, 4-5).
6. *gv-a-ɣviʒ-eb-da*: *gv-* "us", version vowel *a-*, root, *-eb*, imperfect *-da* "he, she". "Us" has its own prefix, *gv-*, so it needs no *-t*; the *-t* is only for plural "we" and "you" as subject or "you all" as object (lessons 4-3, 4-4).
7. *Gamoacxo* is the aorist of a class 1 verb: series II, so the subject is ergative (*bebia* is a vowel stem, *-m*) and the object nominative (lessons 4-8, 4-12).
8. The ending *-o* is one of the third-person singular aorist endings; it has no *v-* and no *-e*. "I baked" is *gamovacxve* (Wiktionary; lesson 4-12).
9. *Šemodiodnen* is an imperfect (series I), and "go, come" is class 2, whose subject is nominative in every series anyway (lessons 4-9, 4-11).
10. *še-v-ɣeb-e*: preverb *še-*, *v-* "I", root (thematic suffix *-av* dropped), aorist ending *-e* "I/you" (lesson 4-12).
11. *C̣avalt* is the future of "go off": the stem changes (*-di-* → *-val*) instead of adding a preverb, because the verb of motion already has one in the present (lesson 4-11).
12. *ga-gv-a-ɣviʒ-eb-s*: preverb *ga-*, object *gv-* "us", version vowel, root, *-eb*, *-s* "she". The future: preverb + present (lessons 4-3, 4-6).
13. Conditional: the future *davrčebi* + the class 2 imperfect ending *-odi*. *Rčeba* is class 2 (lessons 4-7, 4-10).
14. Nino is going away from the speaker, so *mi-*. If she were coming towards the speaker, *modixar* and *saidan modixar?* "where are you coming from?" would fit (lesson 4-11).
15. *Bebia namcxvars gamoacxobda, magram šakari ar aris.* Conditional = future + *-da*; series I cases (lesson 4-7).
16. *Me šen gxedav, šen ḳi me ar mxedav.* "I see you": *g-* takes the slot and *v-* is dropped; "you see me": *m-*, no suffix (lessons 4-3, 4-4).
17. *Bavšvebi ezoši xaṭavdnen, mama ḳi ɣobes ašenebda.* Imperfect: nominative subject, dative object. In the aorist: *mamam ḳi ɣobe aašena*: ergative subject, nominative object, the *-eb* gone (lessons 4-5, 4-12).
18. *Axali sḳola momaval c̣els ašendeba.* Class 2 with *-d-*, future preverb *a-*; the school is the nominative subject (lesson 4-10).
19. *C̣erili gušin davc̣eret, xval ḳi gavgzavnit.* Aorist with the object nominative, then the future with the object dative, though it is the same letter: the series decides (lessons 4-6, 4-12).
20. The imperfect is series I, so the subject is nominative: *bebia dilas adre gvaɣviʒebda* (lessons 4-5, 4-8).
21. The aorist needs an ergative subject and a nominative object: *babuam vazi dargo* (lesson 4-12).
22. Only one prefix fits; the object wins: *gxedav* (lesson 4-4).
23. "Go" is class 2: no ergative. *Nino maɣaziaši c̣avida* (lessons 4-9, 4-11).
24. Class 2 "they" is *-ian*: *bavšvebi izrdebian* (lesson 4-10). The class 1 aorist "they" is *-es*, and *-en* is the present ending: *mat c̣erilebi dac̣eres* (lesson 4-12).
