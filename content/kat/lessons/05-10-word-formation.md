## The idea

**Derivation** is making a new word from an existing one with an affix, as English makes *gardener* from *garden* or *joyless* from *joy*. Georgian does this constantly, and with a small set of affixes. Most of them are **circumfixes** (lesson 5-2): one part goes before the stem and one after. Once you know a dozen of them, a large share of the vocabulary becomes transparent.

You have been meeting derived words since part 2 without taking them apart. *Sakartvelo* "Georgia" is *sa-kartvel-o*, "the place for the Georgians". *Mezobeli* "neighbour", *mepe* "king", *metevze* "fisherman" share the *me-* of people and trades. *Bavšvoba* "childhood" is *bavšv-oba*; *tavisupleba* "freedom" is *tavisupal-eba*. *Ḳartveli* "a Georgian" and *ḳartuli* "Georgian (language, thing)" differ only in *-el-i* versus *-ur-i*.

Each affix has a core meaning, set out by Bolkvadze and Kiziria in their tables of derived nouns and adjectives:

- **sa- ... -o** (and *sa- ... -e*): a place or thing *for* something.
- **me- ... -e**: a person whose trade is something.
- **-oba**, **-eba**: an abstract noun, a state or period.
- **-el-i**: a person from a place.
- **-ur-i** (*-ul-i* after an *r*): a thing from a place, or of a kind.
- **si- ... -e** (and *si- ... -o*): the abstract quality of an adjective.
- **u- ... -o** (and *u- ... -ur-i*): lacking something.

One sound rule runs through several of them. Bolkvadze and Kiziria note that when the stem already contains *r*, the *r* of a suffix *-ur* changes to *l*: *kart-ul-i*, not *\*karturi*; *megruli* "Mingrelian". Georgian avoids two *r*'s close together; the sources state this as a rule of the affixes, not a general law.

## How it is built

**sa- ... -o: "place or thing for".** *Sa-* before the noun or verb stem, *-o* after: *sa-kartvel-o* "Georgia", *sa-mzareul-o* "kitchen" (place for the cook), *sa-avadmq̇op-o* "hospital" (place for the sick), *sa-sc̣avl-o* "educational, for study" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary). With *-e*: *sa-pul-e* "wallet" (for money), *sa-q̇ur-e* "earring" (for the ear). With *-ur-*: *sa-ta-ur-i* "title" (for the head). Because the result ends in *-o* or *-e*, it is a vowel stem.

**me- ... -e: trades.** *Me-baɣ-e* "gardener" (*baɣi* "garden"), *me-ṗur-e* "baker", *me-ḳar-e* "doorman, goalkeeper" (*ḳari* "door") (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary). You know *metevze* "fisherman" and *mezobeli* "neighbour".

**-oba: abstract nouns, states, periods.** Add *-oba* to a noun or adjective: *bavšv-oba* "childhood", *axalgazrd-oba* "youth", *mšvid-oba* "peace" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria). After a vowel + *v*, the *v* drops: *tavi* → *ta-oba* "generation". *-Eba* does the same job for other stems: *bednier-eba* "happiness", *ʒlier-eba* "strength".

**-el-i: people from a place.** *Kartv-el-i* "a Georgian", *inglis-el-i*, *esṗan-el-i* (lesson 2-2), *ḳaxeti* → *ḳax-el-i* "person from Kakheti".

**-ur-i / -ul-i: things from a place, kinds.** *Kart-ul-i* "Georgian (thing, language)", *inglis-ur-i* "English (language)", *ḳavḳasi-ur-i* "Caucasian" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria). People take *-el-i*; things take *-ur-i*. The same split is behind *esṗaneli* "a Spaniard" and *esṗanuri* "Spanish" (lesson 5-6).

**si- ... -e (-o): the quality itself.** Take an adjective's stem: *si-lamaz-e* "beauty" (*lamazi*), *si-maɣl-e* "height" (*maɣali*, with syncope), *si-mdidr-e* "wealth" (*mdidari*), *si-mc̣ar-e* "bitterness" (*mc̣are*), *si-tb-o* "warmth" (*tbili*) (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary). The *si-* of *sicili* "laughter" and *sixaruli* "joy" (lesson 5-8) is the same prefix in a masdar circumfix.

**u- ... -o (-ur-i, -ul-i): "without".** *U-ḳanon-o* "lawless, illegal" (*ḳanoni*), *u-gem-ur-i* "tasteless" (*gemo* "taste"), *u-per-ul-i* "colourless" (*peri*, so *-ul-* after the *r*) (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary). *U-bed-ur-i* "unhappy, unlucky" is "without fate, without luck" (*bedi*, lesson 4-16).

**Two more for recognition.** *-ian-i* "having": *mz-ian-i* "sunny"; *nič̣ieri* "talented" has *-ier-i* with the same meaning. *-del-i* "of a time": *gušin-del-i* "yesterday's", *šaršan-del-i* "last year's", *dɣevan-del-i* "today's" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary).

**Glossing.** Derived words are glossed as whole words: *mebaɣe* `gardener`. The affixes are taken apart in the notes, not in the gloss line, because they are not inflection.

## Where it applies

- **sa- ... -o / -e / -ur-i**: places (*sakartvelo*, *samzareulo*, *saavadmq̇opo*, *sasṭumro*), tools and containers (*sapule*, *saq̇ure*, *satauri*), "for" adjectives (*sasc̣avlo*, *sainṭereso* "interesting", lesson 3-10). Country names: *sakartvelo*, *saprangeti* "France" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria).
- **me- ... -e (-ur-i)**: trades and roles (*mebaɣe*, *meṗure*, *meḳare*, *metevze*, *mepe*).
- **-oba / -eba**: periods (*bavšvoba*, *axalgazrdoba*), states (*mšvidoba*, *tavisupleba*, *bedniereba*), and the masdars of *-ob* and *-eb* verbs (lesson 5-8), which look the same.
- **-el-i**: people by origin (*kartveli*, *ḳaxeli*, *ingliseli*).
- **-ur-i / -ul-i**: languages, foods and things by origin (*kartuli*, *inglisuri*, *ḳaxuri*), and kinds (*ḳavḳasiuri*).
- **si- ... -e / -o**: qualities from adjectives (*silamaze*, *simaɣle*, *simdidre*, *sitbo*); also in masdars (*sicili*, *siq̇varuli*).
- **u- ... -o / -ur-i / -ul-i**: "without" (*uḳanono*, *ugemuri*, *uperuli*, *ubeduri*).
- **-ian-i, -ier-i**: "having" (*mziani*, *nič̣ieri*).
- **-del-i**: "of (a time)" (*gušindeli*, *šaršandeli*, *dɣevandeli*).

## Watch out

**Do not use -ur-i for people.** *\*Kartuli ḳaci* says "a Georgian-style man" at best. People are *-el-i*: *kartveli ḳaci*. Things and languages are *-ur-i*: *kartuli ɣvino*.

**Watch the r → l change.** *\*Karturi*, *\*uperuri* keep a second *r*. After an *r* in the stem, *-ur* becomes *-ul*: *kartuli*, *uperuli* (Bolkvadze and Kiziria).

**sa- ... -o is not the future participle.** *Sac̣eri* "for writing" (lesson 5-7) and *sasc̣avlo* "educational" both have *sa-*, but the participle is built on a verb and keeps its object in the genitive; the *sa- ... -o* word is a fixed noun or adjective. Read the ending: *-i* or *-el-i* for the participle, *-o* or *-e* here.

**Me- is not "I".** *Mebaɣe* is not "I, the garden": *me-* here is a derivational prefix, like the *me-* of ordinals (*mesame*, lesson 5-4).

**Derived words in -o and -e are vowel stems.** *\*Sakartvelois* treats *sakartvelo* as a consonant stem. Decline these words by the vowel-stem rules of lesson 3-5: *sakartvelos* "of Georgia".

**Not every combination exists.** These affixes are productive but not free, and the vowel (*-e* or *-o*, *-oba* or *-eba*) is fixed for each word. Check a dictionary before coining.

**U- ... -o does not negate verbs.** For "not done" use the privative participle *u-* ... *-el-i* (lesson 5-7): *dauc̣ereli*, not *\*uc̣ero*.

## Connections

Spanish *-ero* (*panadero*, *portero*) does the work of *me-* ... *-e*, and *-dad* (*belleza*, *altura*) the work of *si-* ... *-e*. Arabic builds "place of" nouns with a prefix *ma-* (*maktab* "office", *madrasa* "school"), which is a close functional match for *sa-* ... *-o*. Georgian *-el-i* versus *-ur-i* matches French *un Français* versus *français* only partly: French uses one word for both, Georgian two.

## Exercises

1. Take apart *samzareulo* and say what it literally means.
2. Make the trade name from *ṗuri* "bread" and translate it.
3. What is the difference between *kartveli* and *kartuli*?
4. Make the abstract nouns from *lamazi* and *mdidari*.
5. Make "lawless" from *ḳanoni*.
6. Find and fix the error: *\*Es kartveli ɣvinoa.*
7. Translate: "Yesterday's weather was sunny."
8. Why is "Georgian language" *kartuli* and not *\*karturi*?

## Worked answers

1. *Sa-mzareul-o*: *sa-* ... *-o* "place for" + *mzareuli* "cook": "the place for the cook", a kitchen.
2. *Me-ṗur-e* "baker, breadmaker" (Bolkvadze and Kiziria; Wiktionary).
3. *Kartveli* is a Georgian person (*-el-i*); *kartuli* is a Georgian thing or the language (*-ul-i*).
4. *Si-* ... *-e*: *silamaze* "beauty", *simdidre* "wealth" (with syncope of *mdidar-*).
5. *U-* ... *-o*: *uḳanono* "lawless, illegal".
6. Wine is a thing, not a person: *Es kartuli ɣvinoa.*
7. *Gušindeli amindi mziani iq̇o.* *Iq̇o* "was" (lesson 4-25).
8. The stem *kart-* is followed by *-ur*, and after an *r* in the stem *-ur* becomes *-ul* (Bolkvadze and Kiziria).
