## How to use these tables

These are the paradigm tables of the guide, collected in one place. Each was first given at the end of the lesson that teaches it, after the pattern had been explained; the source line of each table names that lesson, so you can go back to the explanation. Nothing here is new.

Forms are in this guide's spelling, with macrons on long vowels (part 1). The case order is nominative, vocative, accusative, genitive, dative, ablative. Where sources differ on a form, the lesson says so; the most common such points are the i-stem endings of the third declension (part 3, lesson 8) and a few hidden quantities (dossier §6).
