## How to use these tables

These are the verb tables of the guide, collected from the lessons that teach them; each table's source line names its lesson. The model verbs are *amō* (first conjugation), *moneō* or *videō* (second), *regō* (third), *capiō* (third -iō) and *audiō* (fourth), with *sum* beside them.

To find a form, first decide which stem it is built on (present, perfect or supine), then which tense or mood the marker shows, then the person ending (part 4, lesson 1). The last tables summarise the uses of the subjunctive in subordinate clauses, as taught in part 6, and the impersonal verbs of part 7.
