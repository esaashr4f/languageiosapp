## Terms

Each term is defined where it first appears; the lesson is given in brackets.

- **-iō verb**: A third-conjugation verb like capiō that keeps an i before some endings (lat-04-02).
- **ablative absolute**: A noun and participle in the ablative giving the background to the main clause (lat-06-04).
- **ablative of comparison**: The ablative meaning 'than' after a comparative (lat-05-04).
- **accusative**: The case of the object (lat-02-03).
- **active**: The voice in which the subject does the action (lat-04-11).
- **adjective**: A word that describes a noun (lat-02-07).
- **adverb**: A word describing how, when or where (lat-05-03).
- **agent**: The doer in a passive sentence, ā + ablative (lat-04-11).
- **agreement**: Two words matching in features such as number, gender and case (lat-02-02).
- **anaphora**: Defined in lat-07-06.
- **anceps**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **antecedent**: The noun a relative clause describes (lat-06-02).
- **antepenult**: The third-from-last syllable of a word (lat-01-04).
- **apex**: A mark Romans sometimes wrote over a long vowel in inscriptions (lat-01-05).
- **apodosis**: The main clause of a condition (lat-06-13).
- **aspect**: How an action is viewed: as a whole and completed, or as ongoing (lat-04-07).
- **assimilation**: A sound changing to match its neighbour, as ad- + capiō becoming accipiō (lat-04-16).
- **asyndeton**: Leaving out 'and' between items (lat-05-06).
- **attributive**: Of an adjective: attached directly to a noun, as in 'the good girl' (lat-02-07).
- **caesura**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **cardinal**: A counting number: one, two (lat-05-05).
- **case**: The form of a noun that shows its job in the sentence (lat-02-02).
- **causal clause**: A clause saying why (lat-06-03).
- **chiasmus**: A crossed arrangement of words in the pattern A B B A (lat-02-08).
- **clause of comparison**: An 'as if' clause (lat-06-14).
- **clause of proviso**: A 'provided that' clause (lat-06-14).
- **cognomen**: Defined in lat-07-11.
- **cognōmen**: The name of a branch of a family, often a nickname in origin (lat-07-11).
- **colloquial Latin**: Defined in lat-07-12.
- **comparative**: The 'more, -er' form (lat-05-04).
- **complementary infinitive**: An infinitive completing a verb like possum or volō (lat-04-03).
- **compound verb**: A verb made from a prefix and a simple verb (lat-04-16).
- **concessive clause**: An 'although' clause (lat-06-14).
- **conditional sentence**: An 'if' sentence (lat-06-13).
- **conjugation**: A family of verbs that share one pattern of endings (lat-02-01).
- **conjunction**: A joining word (lat-05-06).
- **coordinating conjunction**: A conjunction joining equal parts: et, sed, aut (lat-05-06).
- **dactyl**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **dactylic hexameter**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **dative of direction**: Defined in lat-07-09.
- **dative of purpose**: The dative of what something serves as (lat-05-09).
- **dative of the agent**: Defined in lat-07-02.
- **declension**: A family of nouns that share one pattern of endings (lat-02-02).
- **defective verb**: A verb lacking some forms, as inquit or meminī (lat-04-15).
- **degrees**: The positive, comparative and superlative forms of an adjective (lat-05-04).
- **demonstrative**: A pointing word: this, that (lat-03-11).
- **deponent**: A verb with passive endings and active meaning (lat-04-13).
- **derivative**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **diminutive**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **diphthong**: Two vowel sounds glided together inside one syllable, as in ae and au (lat-01-02).
- **distributive**: A number meaning 'so many each': singulī, bīnī (lat-05-05).
- **double dative**: A dative of purpose together with a dative of the person concerned (lat-05-09).
- **dual**: A form for exactly two; in Latin only relics such as duo, ambō (lat-05-05).
- **Early (Old) Latin**: Defined in lat-07-12.
- **Early Latin**: The Latin of the oldest inscriptions, Plautus and Ennius, before the classical standard (lat-07-12).
- **ecclesiastical pronunciation**: Defined in lat-07-13.
- **edition**: Defined in lat-07-14.
- **elegiac couplet**: Defined in lat-07-09.
- **elision**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **enclitic**: A small word that leans on the word before it and is written joined to it, like -ne and -que (lat-02-06).
- **ending**: The final, changeable piece of a word that says what job it is doing (lat-00-02).
- **epigraphy**: Defined in lat-07-14.
- **fear clause**: A clause saying what is feared (lat-06-10).
- **foot**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **frequentative**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **fusional**: Built so that one ending carries several meanings at once (lat-00-02).
- **future**: The tense of an action still to come (lat-04-05).
- **future imperative**: A command form for later or standing orders, in -tō (lat-04-09).
- **future participle**: The participle in -ūrus meaning 'about to' (lat-05-07).
- **future perfect**: The tense of an action that will be complete before a future moment: will have loved (lat-04-08).
- **gender**: The class a noun belongs to (masculine, feminine, neuter), which decides the endings of words that agree with it (lat-02-04).
- **gerund**: Defined in lat-07-01.
- **gerundive**: Defined in lat-07-02.
- **gloss**: The line under an example that labels each piece of each word (lat-00-02).
- **grammatical gender**: Gender as a class of words, regardless of sex (lat-02-04).
- **Greek accusative**: Defined in lat-07-09.
- **heavy syllable**: A syllable with a long vowel or diphthong, or closed by a consonant (lat-01-03).
- **hendecasyllable**: A lyric line of eleven syllables in a fixed pattern, used by Catullus (lat-08-04).
- **hiatus**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **hyperbaton**: Defined in lat-07-06.
- **i-stem**: A third-declension noun whose stem once ended in i, which shows in endings such as genitive plural -ium (lat-03-08).
- **imperative**: The command form (lat-04-09).
- **imperfect**: The tense of an action going on or repeated in the past (lat-04-04).
- **impersonal verb**: Defined in lat-07-04.
- **inceptive**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **inclusive counting**: Defined in lat-07-11.
- **indefinite pronoun**: A pronoun meaning someone, anyone, each (lat-05-10).
- **indicative**: The mood of statements and questions of fact (lat-04-09).
- **indirect command**: A reported order or request (lat-06-10).
- **indirect question**: A question embedded as a clause (lat-06-11).
- **indirect statement**: A reported statement, in Latin an accusative and infinitive (ōrātiō oblīqua) (lat-06-05).
- **infinitive**: The form that names the action: to love, amāre (lat-04-03).
- **interrogative**: A question word (lat-06-01).
- **Kalends, Nones, Ides**: Defined in lat-07-11.
- **Leiden conventions**: Defined in lat-07-14.
- **light syllable**: A syllable with a short vowel that is not closed by a consonant (lat-01-03).
- **locative**: A rare case meaning 'at' a place, used with names of towns and a few other nouns (lat-03-01; fully in lat-03-13).
- **macron**: A bar over a vowel marking it as long: ā (lat-01-01).
- **metre**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **middle**: A use of the passive for actions done to or for oneself (lat-04-11).
- **mood**: The form of a verb that shows whether it states, commands or imagines (lat-04-09).
- **natural gender**: Gender that follows the sex of a person or animal (lat-02-04).
- **negation**: Making a sentence say that something is not so (lat-02-06).
- **nomen**: Defined in lat-07-11.
- **nominative**: The case of the subject (lat-02-02).
- **number**: Whether a word refers to one (singular) or more than one (plural) (lat-02-01).
- **numeral adverb**: A number of times: semel, bis (lat-05-05).
- **nōmen**: The name of a Roman's clan (gēns) (lat-07-11).
- **object**: The thing or person acted on (lat-02-03).
- **optative**: Expressing a wish (lat-04-17).
- **ordinal**: A number of order: first, second (lat-05-05).
- **orthography**: A language's spelling system and conventions (lat-01-05).
- **parataxis**: Placing clauses side by side, joined by "and" or by nothing, rather than subordinating one to another (lat-08-02).
- **parisyllabic**: Having the same number of syllables in the nominative and genitive singular, as cīvis, cīvis (lat-03-08).
- **participle**: An adjective made from a verb (lat-04-12).
- **partitive genitive**: The genitive of the whole a part is taken from (lat-05-09).
- **passive**: The voice in which the subject undergoes the action (lat-04-11).
- **pentameter**: Defined in lat-07-09.
- **penult**: The second-from-last syllable of a word (lat-01-04).
- **perfect participle**: The participle in -tus/-sus meaning having been done (lat-04-12).
- **perfect stem**: The stem from the third principal part, which builds the perfect, pluperfect and future perfect (lat-04-06).
- **perfect system**: The tenses built on the perfect stem (lat-04-08).
- **period**: Defined in lat-07-06.
- **periphrastic**: A tense formed with a participle and sum (lat-05-07).
- **person**: Who is acting: the speaker (first), the one spoken to (second), or anyone else (third) (lat-02-01).
- **pluperfect**: The tense of an action complete before another past moment: had loved (lat-04-08).
- **plural**: More than one (lat-02-01).
- **positive**: The plain form of an adjective (lat-05-04).
- **potential**: Expressing what may or would happen (lat-04-19).
- **praenomen**: Defined in lat-07-11.
- **praenōmen**: A Roman's personal first name, usually abbreviated (lat-07-11).
- **predicate adjective**: An adjective that says what the subject is, after a verb like 'be' (lat-02-07).
- **predicate noun**: A noun that says what the subject is, after a verb like 'be' (lat-02-05).
- **preposition**: A small word placed before a noun to show place, direction or relation, such as in, ad, cum (lat-03-04).
- **present participle**: The adjective in -ns meaning '-ing' (lat-05-07).
- **present tense**: The tense for what is happening now or happens generally (lat-02-01).
- **primary tense**: Present, future or future perfect main verb (lat-06-06).
- **pronominal adjective**: An adjective declined partly like a pronoun, with genitive -īus and dative -ī: ūnus, tōtus (lat-05-02).
- **pronoun**: A word that stands in for a noun: I, you, he (lat-03-10).
- **protasis**: The 'if' clause (lat-06-13).
- **purpose clause**: A clause saying what something is done for (final clause) (lat-06-07).
- **question particle**: A small word that turns a statement into a question (lat-02-06).
- **reflexive**: Referring back to the subject of its clause: himself, herself, themselves (lat-03-10).
- **relative clause**: A clause describing a noun, opened by quī (lat-06-02).
- **relative clause of characteristic**: A relative clause in the subjunctive describing the kind of person or thing (lat-06-15).
- **relative pronoun**: quī, quae, quod: 'who, which' (lat-06-02).
- **repraesentātiō**: Defined in lat-07-05.
- **restored pronunciation**: The pronunciation of educated Romans of the classical period, reconstructed from ancient evidence (lat-01-01).
- **result clause**: A clause saying what followed (consecutive clause) (lat-06-09).
- **rhotacism**: The change of s to r between vowels in early Latin, which explains pairs like corpus, corporis (lat-03-07).
- **root**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **scansion**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **secondary tense**: A past main verb: imperfect, perfect or pluperfect (lat-06-06).
- **semi-deponent**: A verb active in the present system and deponent in the perfect, as audeō, ausus sum (lat-04-13).
- **senarius**: The iambic trimeter, a line of six iambic feet with many substitutions (lat-08-05).
- **sequence of tenses**: The rule matching a subordinate subjunctive's tense to the main verb's (lat-06-06).
- **singular**: One (lat-02-01).
- **spondee**: Defined in lat-07-08.
- **stem**: The part of a word that stays the same while the ending changes (lat-00-02).
- **stop**: A consonant made by blocking the air completely and releasing it: p, t, c, b, d, g (lat-01-03).
- **stress**: The extra force that makes one syllable of a word stand out (lat-01-04).
- **subject**: The doer, or the thing the sentence is about (lat-02-02).
- **subjunctive**: The mood of what is wished, intended or imagined (lat-04-17).
- **subordinate clause**: A clause that depends on another (lat-06-03).
- **subordinating conjunction**: A word that opens a subordinate clause, such as cum or ut (lat-06-03).
- **substantive**: An adjective used as a noun: bonī 'good men' (lat-05-02).
- **substantive clause**: A clause standing as subject or object of a verb (lat-06-09).
- **suffix**: Defined in lat-07-10.
- **superlative**: The 'most, -est, very' form (lat-05-04).
- **supine**: Defined in lat-07-03.
- **suppletive**: Built from more than one unrelated root, as ferō, tulī, lātum (lat-04-14).
- **syllable**: A beat of a word, built round one vowel or diphthong (lat-01-03).
- **synchysis**: Defined in lat-07-06.
- **temporal clause**: A clause saying when (lat-06-03).
- **tense**: The form of a verb that places an action in time (lat-02-01).
- **thematic vowel**: The linking vowel between a verb stem and its ending, as the -i- of regit (lat-04-02).
- **tmesis**: Defined in lat-07-09.
- **transitive**: Of a verb: taking an object (lat-02-03).
- **vocative**: The form used to call or address someone (lat-03-06).
- **voice**: Whether the subject does or undergoes the action (lat-04-11).
- **ōrātiō oblīqua**: Defined in lat-07-05.
- **ōrātiō rēcta**: Defined in lat-07-05.
