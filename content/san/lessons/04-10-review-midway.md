## Before you start

This review covers the first half of part 4: the present classes 1, 4, 6 and 10 (san-04-01), the middle voice (san-04-02), the imperfect and the augment (san-04-03), the verbal prefixes (san-04-04), the imperative (san-04-05), the optative (san-04-06), the joins of final consonants (san-04-07), the root class (san-04-08) and the reduplicating class (san-04-09). It assumes the cases and stems of part 3.

The passage retells, in short constructed sentences, the opening of a famous Hitopadeśa story: the pigeons who escaped a hunter's net by flying up together. The last line is an attested line from the Nala story. Translate each line before you look at the answers; every answer names the lesson behind the point.

## Exercises

1. Translate line 1. Why is *vṛkṣa* written without *-ḥ*, and why does *mahān* not change before *v*?
2. Translate line 2. Split *nyavasan* and explain the *y*.
3. Translate line 3. Undo the join *āgacchaj jālaṃ*, and split *nyadadhāt*: which class is its verb, and why does it begin *dadhā-*?
4. Translate line 4. Explain why *apaśyan* and *taṇḍulān* do not change, undo *lobhād*, and split *avātaran*.
5. Translate line 5. What is the avagraha standing for?
6. Translate line 6. Name the two verb forms, their classes, persons and moods, and explain *rājābravīd*.
7. Translate line 7. How does the imperative 1PL differ from the present?
8. Translate line 8. Explain *sahodapatañ* in three steps.
9. Translate line 9. What is *bhṛśam*, and why is the verb active?
10. Translate line 10. Give the form, voice and mood of *yateran*, and say why the optative suits a moral.
11. Translate line 11. Which word belongs to the narration and which to Damayantī's speech?
12. Put line 2 into the present tense.
13. Put line 6's command into the optative, as general advice: "one should give up fear".
14. Build "The pigeons were afraid of the hunter" with √bhī (imperfect 3PL).
15. Build "The king gives rice to the pigeons" with √dā.
16. Build "The hunter sits under (= at the foot of) the tree and sleeps": *vṛkṣamūle* "at the foot of the tree", √ās and √svap.
17. Build "Let him eat the fruit" with √ad.
18. Find and fix the error: *\*vyādho jālaṃ nidadhāt* for "the hunter put down the net".
19. Find and fix the error: *\*kapotā bhayaṃ labhanti*.
20. Find and fix the error: *\*vyādhāḥ kapotān hananti*.
21. Find and fix the error: *\*te vṛkṣād phalaṃ khādanti*.
22. Find and fix the error: *\*dhanino yācakebhyo dhanaṃ dadanti*.

## Worked answers

1. "Once there was a great tree in a forest." *vṛkṣaḥ* before a vowel loses its *-ḥ* and leaves a gap (san-02-06, san-03-07). *mahān*: final *-n* changes only before *c, ch, ṭ, ṭh, t, th, j, ś, l* and, after a short vowel, before vowels; before *v* it stands (san-04-07). *āsīt* is the imperfect of √as (san-04-03).
2. "The pigeons lived there happily." *ni-a-vasa-n*: prefix *ni*, augment, stem *vasa-* (class 1), 3PL *-n*. *ni* before the augment's *a* becomes *ny* (san-04-04, san-03-07).
3. "One day a hunter came to the forest and laid a net on the ground." *āgacchaj jālaṃ* is *āgacchat jālam*: *-t* before *j* becomes *-j* (san-04-07). *ni-a-dadhā-t*: √dhā is class 3, reduplicated with the unaspirated *d* (san-04-09); the augment sits after the prefix (san-04-04).
4. "The pigeons saw the grains of rice and, out of greed, came down to the ground." Final *-n* does not change before *bh* or after a long vowel before a vowel (*taṇḍulān apaśyan*) (san-04-07). *lobhād* is *lobhāt* before a vowel. *avātaran* = *ava-a-tara-n*, imperfect of *ava-tṝ*, the augment merging with the *a* of *ava* (san-04-04).
5. "They fell into the net." The avagraha stands for the *a* of the augment, lost after *te* (san-03-07): *te apatan* → *te 'patan*.
6. "The king of the pigeons said: 'Give up fear!'" *abravīt*: √brū, class 2, imperfect 3SG active, with *ī* (san-04-08). *tyajata*: √tyaj, class 1, imperative 2PL (san-04-05). *rājā* + *abravīt* → *rājābravīt* (san-03-07), and *-t* before *bh* is *-d* (san-04-07).
7. "Let us fly up together." The imperative 1PL is *-āma*; the present is *-āmaḥ* (san-04-05). *saha* + *ut-* → *sahot-* (san-03-07).
8. "They flew up together and carried off the net." (a) *ud-a-patan*: augment after the prefix, *ud* keeping *d* before a vowel (san-04-04). (b) *saha* + *udapatan* → *sahodapatan* (san-03-07). (c) *-n* before *j* → *-ñ* (san-04-07).
9. "And the hunter grieved bitterly." *bhṛśam* "greatly" (san-04-03 vocabulary). √śuc is active in the guide's vocabulary (*śocati*, san-02-03), so its imperfect is active (san-04-02).
10. "Friends should strive together." *yateran*: √yat, middle only (san-04-02), optative 3PL middle *-eran* (san-04-06). A moral states what ought to be: the optative of rules.
11. "Then she said to the swan there: 'Speak thus to Nala too.'" *abravīt* is the narration (imperfect, san-04-03); *vada* is Damayantī's command (imperative 2SG, the bare stem, san-04-05). *api* before *e* is *apy* (san-03-07).
12. *tatra kapotāḥ sukhena nivasanti*. The prefix stays; the augment goes; *-n* becomes *-nti* (san-04-03, san-04-04).
13. *bhayaṃ tyajet*. Optative 3SG *-et*, no subject: a general rule (san-04-06).
14. *kapotā vyādhād abibhayuḥ*. Imperfect 3PL of class 3 has *-uḥ*, with guṇa before it (san-04-09); the thing feared in the ablative (san-03-03); *vyādhāt* before a vowel → *vyādhād*.
15. *nṛpaḥ kapotebhyas taṇḍulān dadāti*. Strong *dadā-* in the 3SG (san-04-09); dative plural; *-aḥ* before *t* → *-as* (san-02-06).
16. *vyādho vṛkṣamūla āste svapiti ca*. √ās is middle, class 2: *āste* (san-04-08); √svap: *svapiti*. *vṛkṣamūle* before *ā* → *vṛkṣamūla* (*e* before a vowel becomes *a*, san-03-07).
17. *phalam attu*. √ad, class 2, imperative 3SG strong *at-tu* (san-04-08).
18. The past needs the augment after the prefix: *vyādho jālaṃ nyadadhāt* (san-04-04).
19. √labh is middle only: *kapotā bhayaṃ labhante* (san-04-02).
20. √han before a vowel is *ghn-*: *vyādhāḥ kapotān ghnanti* (san-04-08). Here *-āḥ* before *k* keeps the visarga (san-02-06).
21. *ph* is voiceless: *vṛkṣāt phalaṃ khādanti* (san-04-07).
22. Class 3 has no *n* in the 3PL: *dadati* (san-04-09).
