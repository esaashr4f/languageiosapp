## Before you start

This review teaches nothing new. It covers the first half of part 6: relatives with *amba-* and the o of reference (lesson 6.1), the relative marker inside the verb with *-li-*, *-na-*, *-taka-* (6.2), object relatives and the object prefix (6.3), the general and negative relatives (6.4), the relatives of "be" and "have" (6.5), "when, where, how" with *-po-*, *-ko-*, *-mo-*, *-vyo-* (6.6), and focus with *ndi-* and *si-* (6.7). Everything from parts 2 to 5 is assumed.

The passage is a short story constructed for this review. For every relative form, ask three questions: which noun does the relative marker point to? Who is the subject of the relative verb? Is there an object prefix, and why?

## Exercises

1. Translate the passage into natural English without looking at the translation lines.
2. In line 1, rewrite *aliyekuja* with *amba-*.
3. In line 2, explain the three class 9 and class 10 pieces in *aliyoipoteza* and *alizokuwa nazo*.
4. In line 2, why *alizokuwa nazo* and not *alizo nazo*?
5. In line 3, what would *alipoipoteza* mean instead of *alivyoipoteza*?
6. In line 4, rewrite *wasiojua* as a positive general relative ("people who know").
7. In line 5, could *ambayo aliiokota* be one word? Give it.
8. In line 6, say instead "it wasn't this boy who picked it up".
9. Say "the guests who have arrived are waiting".
10. Say "the book which I will read".
11. Say "the students who don't eat meat".
12. Say "this is the town where I was born".
13. Say "the friend I travelled with".
14. Say "the ship which is in the port".
15. Correct *\*Kitabu niliyesoma ni kizuri.*
16. Correct *\*Wageni waliomefika wanasubiri.*
17. Improve *Sijui wapi alikwenda* with a relative.
18. Correct *\*Watoto wasiokuli.*
19. Correct *\*Mtalii atayekuja kesho.*
20. Correct *\*Pesa nilizo na ni chache.*

## Worked answers

1. "Yesterday a tourist who had come from Europe lost her purse. The purse she lost had all the money she had. She went to the police station and explained how she had lost it. The officer who listened to her said, 'People who don't know this town lose things every day.' An hour later, a boy brought a purse which he had picked up at the market. The officer said, 'This boy is the one who picked it up.' The tourist gave the boy a present and said, 'This is how good people behave.'" (Lessons 6.1 to 6.7.)
2. *Mtalii ambaye alikuja kutoka Ulaya.* *Ambaye* for a person; the verb is an ordinary past. Lessons 6.1, 6.2.
3. In *a-li-yo-i-poteza*, *a-* is the tourist, *-yo-* (class 9) points to *pochi*, and *-i-* is the class 9 object prefix for the same purse. In *a-li-zo-kuwa na-zo*, *-zo-* points to *pesa* (class 10) and *nazo* repeats it after *na*. Lessons 6.3, 6.5.
4. *Alizo nazo* is present: "which she has". The story is in the past, so the relative of *kuwa* is used: *alizokuwa nazo* "which she had". Lesson 6.5.
5. "She explained **when** she had lost it." *-po-* is time; *-vyo-* is manner. Lesson 6.6.
6. *Watu wajuao mji huu.* No tense; the relative at the end. Lesson 6.4.
7. Yes: *aliyoiokota*, with *-yo-* after *-li-*. Lessons 6.2, 6.3.
8. *Mvulana huyu siye aliyeiokota.* *Siye* denies the focus. Lesson 6.7.
9. *Wageni ambao wamefika wanasubiri.* *-me-* takes no relative marker. Lessons 6.1, 6.2.
10. *Kitabu nitakachokisoma* (or *nitakachosoma*). Future *-taka-*, class 7 *-cho-*, optional object prefix *-ki-*. Lessons 6.2, 6.3.
11. *Wanafunzi wasiokula nyama.* Negative relative; *ku* stays. Lesson 6.4.
12. *Huu ndio mji nilimozaliwa* (or *nilipozaliwa*). *Ndio* class 3 for *mji*; *-mo-* "in which". Lessons 6.6, 6.7.
13. *Rafiki niliyesafiri naye.* *Naye* repeats the person after "with". Lesson 6.3.
14. *Meli iliyo bandarini.* The relative of "be", class 9. Lesson 6.5.
15. The relative marker points to the book: *-cho-*, not *-ye-*. *Kitabu nilichosoma ni kizuri.* Lesson 6.3.
16. No relative marker after *-me-*: *wageni ambao wamefika*. Lesson 6.2.
17. "Where" inside a statement is normally a relative: *Sijui alikokwenda.* Lesson 6.6.
18. The negative relative keeps final *-a* and *ku*: *watoto wasiokula*. Lesson 6.4.
19. The future before a relative marker is *-taka-*: *mtalii atakayekuja kesho*. Lesson 6.2.
20. *Na* needs its reference form: *Pesa nilizo nazo ni chache.* Lesson 6.5.
