## Before you start

This review covers the first half of part 3: the accusative *-(y)I* for specific objects, with vowel loss and doubling (tur-03-01); the dative *-(y)A* for goals and receivers (tur-03-02); the ablative *-DAn* for starting points, routes and causes (tur-03-03); the possessive suffixes (tur-03-04); the genitive *-(n)In* and the genitive-possessive phrase (tur-03-05); and the *n* between a 3rd-person possessive and a case (tur-03-06). It also uses everything from part 2: nominal sentences, personal endings, *değil*, *mI*, *var* and *yok*, the locative, word order, *-(I)yor* and *-mA*.

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread if you went wrong.

## Passage

**Ayşe writes about her day.** A constructed passage, using only words and grammar taught so far. One new word is glossed in place: *bazen* "sometimes".

1. Benim adım Ayşe. Ankara'danım, ama şimdi İstanbul'da yaşıyorum.
2. Annem ve babam hâlâ Ankara'da. Babam öğretmen, annem doktor.
3. Ağabeyim Almanya'da çalışıyor. Onun eşi Alman.
4. Her sabah evden çıkıyorum ve otobüse biniyorum.
5. Otobüs bir köprüden geçiyor. Pencereden denizi görüyorum.
6. Üniversitenin kütüphanesinde çalışıyorum. Kütüphanenin müdürü çok iyi bir insan.
7. Akşam eve dönüyorum. Bazen arkadaşlarım evime geliyor.
8. Bu akşam Mehmet'e bir hediye veriyorum. Hediyeyi çantama koyuyorum.
9. Mehmet romanlardan hoşlanıyor. Hediyesi bir roman.

## Exercises

1. Translate sentences 1 to 3.
2. Translate sentences 4 to 6.
3. Translate sentences 7 to 9.
4. In sentence 1, take apart *Ankara'danım* and say why there is no buffer *y*.
5. In sentence 1, why does Ayşe say *Benim adım* and not only *Adım*?
6. In sentence 3, why *onun eşi* and not *\*o eşi*? Could *onun* be left out?
7. In sentence 4, explain the cases on *evden* and *otobüse*.
8. In sentence 5, why is it *bir köprüden* but *denizi*? What would *deniz görüyorum* mean?
9. In sentence 6, take apart *kütüphanesinde* piece by piece.
10. In sentence 7, compare *eve* and *evime*.
11. In sentence 8, why is it *bir hediye* in the first sentence and *hediyeyi* in the second?
12. In sentence 9, what case does *hoşlanmak* take? Rewrite the sentence with *sevmek*.
13. Say "My brother's wife works at a hospital."
14. Say "I'm putting my key in his pocket."
15. Say "We're getting off the plane and going to the city centre."
16. Say "Do you (polite) know the name of this river?"
17. Say "Is this umbrella yours (one friend)? — No, it's Ali's."
18. Correct *\*Öğretmeni bakıyorum* ("I'm looking at the teacher").
19. Correct *\*Ali'nin arabasıda iki çanta var* ("There are two bags in Ali's car").
20. Correct *\*Kapı açıyorum ve anahtarım çantaya koyuyorum* ("I'm opening the door and putting my key in the bag").
21. Correct *\*Şehiri ve şehirin insanlarını seviyorum* ("I love the city and the city's people").
22. Correct *\*Kitapdan bir sayfa okuyorum* ("I'm reading a page of the book").

## Worked answers

1. "My name is Ayşe. I'm from Ankara, but now I live in Istanbul. My mother and father are still in Ankara. My father is a teacher, my mother is a doctor. My elder brother works in Germany. His wife is German." The ablative as a predicate says where someone is from (tur-03-03); the locative as a predicate where they are (tur-02-06). Jobs and nationalities are bare predicates (tur-02-01).
2. "Every morning I leave the house and get on the bus. The bus goes over a bridge. From the window I see the sea. I work in the university library. The library's director is a very nice person." *-(I)yor* for a current habit (tur-02-08). *Üniversitenin kütüphanesi* and *kütüphanenin müdürü* are genitive-possessive phrases (tur-03-05).
3. "In the evening I go back home. Sometimes my friends come to my house. This evening I'm giving Mehmet a present. I'm putting the present in my bag. Mehmet likes novels. His present is a novel." *Veriyorum* with *bu akşam* is a plan for later today, as *-(I)yor* allows (tur-02-08).
4. *Ankara-dan-ım*: "Ankara", the ablative *-dan* after the back vowel *a*, the personal ending *-(y)Im* as *-ım*. The ablative ends in *n*, a consonant, so the buffer *y* is not needed (tur-02-02, tur-03-03).
5. She is introducing herself and opening a new topic; Göksel and Kerslake list this as one of the uses of a genitive pronoun (goksel2005 §18.1.5.1). *Adım Ayşe* alone is also correct and more neutral (tur-03-05).
6. The genitive of *o* is *onun*, with the *n* that *o* shows before any suffix (tur-03-05). *O eşi* would read "that spouse of his". *Onun* could be left out, *Eşi Alman*, because *-i* already says "his"; it is there to shift attention from Ayşe to her brother (tur-03-05).
7. *Evden*: the ablative of the place left; *çıkmak* takes it where English has a direct object (tur-03-03). *Otobüse*: the dative of the vehicle boarded; *binmek* takes it (tur-03-02).
8. *Bir köprüden*: the ablative of the route with *geçmek*, and *bir* because the bridge is new to the reader (tur-03-03). *Denizi*: the sea is a particular, known thing, so it is a definite object in the accusative (tur-03-01). *Deniz görüyorum* would treat the sea as a kind of thing, "I see sea", which suits an answer to "what can you see?" but not a sentence about the one sea everyone knows.
9. *Kütüphane-si-nde*: "library", the 3SG possessive *-si* after a vowel (the library *of the university*), then the locative, which starts with *n* after a 3rd-person possessive and keeps *d* after *n* (tur-03-04, tur-03-06).
10. *Eve* is "home" as a destination, with no owner (tur-03-02). *Evime* is "to my house": possessive *-(I)m*, then the dative, with no *n* because the possessive is 1st person (tur-03-06).
11. In the first sentence the present is new, so it is indefinite and bare after *bir*, next to the verb. In the second it is the present just mentioned, a known thing, so it is in the accusative (tur-03-01).
12. The ablative (tur-03-03). With *sevmek*, which takes the accusative: *Mehmet romanları seviyor*. The plural object meaning "novels in general" must take the accusative (goksel2005 §22.4.3; tur-03-01).
13. *Ağabeyimin eşi bir hastanede çalışıyor.* *Ağabeyim* "my brother" becomes the owner in the genitive, *ağabeyimin*; *eş* takes the 3SG possessive. No *n* appears, because *-(I)m* is 1st person (tur-03-05, tur-03-06).
14. *Anahtarımı cebine koyuyorum.* A possessed object in the accusative (tur-03-04); *koymak* takes the dative of the place (tur-03-02); after the 3SG *-i* of *cebi*, the dative starts with *n* (tur-03-06). Out of context *cebine* could also be "your pocket"; add *onun* if needed.
15. *Uçaktan iniyoruz ve şehrin merkezine gidiyoruz.* Ablative with *inmek*, dative of the goal, and the case on the last word of the genitive-possessive phrase, with *n* (tur-03-03, tur-03-05, tur-03-06).
16. *Bu nehrin adını biliyor musunuz?* *Nehir* loses its *i* before the genitive (tur-03-01); *adı* is possessed, so as an object it takes the accusative with *n*: *adını* (tur-03-06); *mu-sunuz* is polite (tur-02-09).
17. *Bu şemsiye senin mi? — Hayır, Ali'nin.* The genitive alone is the predicate; *mI* follows it (tur-03-05, tur-02-05).
18. *Öğretmene bakıyorum.* *Bakmak* takes the dative (tur-03-02).
19. *Ali'nin arabasında iki çanta var.* After *-sı*, the locative is *-nda* (tur-03-06). The noun stays bare after a number (tur-02-03).
20. *Kapıyı açıyorum ve anahtarımı çantaya koyuyorum.* The door is a particular door, so it is accusative (tur-03-01); a possessed object always takes the accusative (tur-03-04); the bag is where the key goes, so dative (tur-03-02).
21. *Şehri ve şehrin insanlarını seviyorum.* *Şehir* loses its second *i* before a vowel, both before the accusative and before the genitive (tur-03-01). The second object is possessed and plural, *insan-lar-ı-nı*, with *n* before the accusative (tur-03-06).
22. *Kitaptan bir sayfa okuyorum.* After voiceless *p* the ablative is *-tan*, and the *p* does not soften before a consonant (tur-03-03). The ablative here marks the whole a part is taken from (goksel2005 §14.5).
