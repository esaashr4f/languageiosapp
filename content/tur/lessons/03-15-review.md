## Before you start

This review covers all of part 3: the accusative (tur-03-01), dative (tur-03-02) and ablative (tur-03-03); possessive suffixes (tur-03-04); the genitive (tur-03-05); the *n* after a 3rd-person possessive (tur-03-06); noun compounds (tur-03-08); "having" with *var* and *yok* (tur-03-09); personal pronouns (tur-03-10); *bu*, *şu*, *o* and *burada*, *orada* (tur-03-11); question words (tur-03-12); *-(y)lA* (tur-03-13); and *-ki* (tur-03-14). The weight is on the second half of the part. Everything from part 2 is assumed.

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread if you went wrong.

## Passage

**A. A visit.** A constructed passage, using only grammar taught so far. Three new words are glossed in place: *ziyaret etmek* "visit" (takes the accusative), *sabahları* "in the mornings", *çünkü* "because".

1. Mehmet'in teyzesi İzmir'de yaşıyor. Mehmet bu hafta onu ziyaret ediyor.
2. Teyzesinin evi denize çok yakın. Evin büyük bir bahçesi var.
3. Bahçedeki ağaçlar çok eski. Teyzesi sabahları bahçede kahve içiyor.
4. Mehmet İstanbul'dan İzmir'e otobüsle geliyor, çünkü uçak bileti çok pahalı.
5. Teyzesi ona soruyor: "Annen nasıl? Kız kardeşin hâlâ Ankara'da mı?"
6. Mehmet: "Annem iyi. Kız kardeşimin yeni bir işi var. Bir bankada çalışıyor."
7. Akşam Mehmet teyzesiyle birlikte şehrin merkezine gidiyor.
8. Bir lokantada balık yiyorlar. Lokantanın sahibi teyzesinin eski bir arkadaşı.
9. Mehmet annesine bir mesaj gönderiyor: "Buradaki balık İstanbul'dakinden güzel!"

**B. A proverb.** *Bir elin nesi var, iki elin sesi var.* (wiktionary, entry *bir elin nesi var iki elin sesi var*.) *Nesi* is *ne* "what" with the 3SG possessive.

## Exercises

1. Translate sentences 1 and 2 of passage A.
2. Translate sentences 3 and 4.
3. Translate sentences 5 and 6.
4. Translate sentences 7 to 9.
5. Translate the proverb in passage B literally, then give its meaning.
6. In sentence 1, take apart *teyzesi* and *onu*. Why is it *onu* and not *\*ou*?
7. In sentence 2, explain *denize yakın* and *Evin büyük bir bahçesi var*.
8. In sentence 3, take apart *bahçedeki* and say why the plain locative *bahçede* would not work before *ağaçlar*.
9. In sentence 4, explain the three suffixes on *İstanbul'dan*, *İzmir'e* and *otobüsle*. What kind of phrase is *uçak bileti*?
10. In sentence 5, why is there no *mI* in *Annen nasıl?* but one in the second question?
11. In sentence 6, take apart *kız kardeşimin* and *işi*.
12. In sentence 7, take apart *teyzesiyle* and *merkezine*. Why does one have *y* and the other *n*?
13. In sentence 9, take apart *İstanbul'dakinden*.
14. In passage B, take apart *elin* and *sesi*.
15. Say "Whose is this bag? — It's mine."
16. Say "The man in the photograph is my grandfather."
17. Ask a stranger "Do you have a passport?" and "Where are you going?"
18. Say "I'm talking to my neighbour's son."
19. Say "Which bus goes to the city centre?"
20. Correct *\*Ali'nin kardeş yok* ("Ali has no brothers or sisters").
21. Correct *\*Arabasınla İzmir'e gidiyor* ("He's going to İzmir in his car").
22. Correct *\*Masada kitabı okuyorum* ("I'm reading the book on the table").
23. Correct *\*Kim arıyorsun mu?* ("Who are you looking for?").
24. Correct *\*Bene bir Türk kahve getiriyor* ("He's bringing me a Turkish coffee").

## Worked answers

1. "Mehmet's aunt lives in İzmir. This week Mehmet is visiting her. His aunt's house is very near the sea. The house has a big garden." *Mehmet'in teyzesi* is a genitive-possessive phrase (tur-03-05); *ziyaret etmek* "visit" takes a direct object, here *onu* (tur-03-10); *yakın* takes the dative (tur-03-02); "having" is the possessive with *var* (tur-03-09).
2. "The trees in the garden are very old. In the mornings his aunt drinks coffee in the garden. Mehmet is coming from Istanbul to İzmir by bus, because the plane ticket is very expensive." *Sabahları* is a time word made from *sabah*; such words come in tur-05-04.
3. "His aunt asks him: 'How's your mother? Is your sister still in Ankara?' Mehmet: 'My mother's well. My sister has a new job. She works at a bank.'" *Ona soruyor*: the person asked is dative (tur-03-02).
4. "In the evening Mehmet goes to the city centre with his aunt. They eat fish at a restaurant. The restaurant's owner is an old friend of his aunt's. Mehmet sends his mother a message: 'The fish here is better than the one in Istanbul!'" *Güzel* with an ablative means "nicer than" even without *daha* "more" (tur-03-03).
5. Literally: "What does one hand have? Two hands have a sound." Meaning: one person alone achieves little; two working together make an impact, like two hands clapping (wiktionary: "two heads are better than one; many hands make light work"). Both halves are "have" sentences: genitive owner, possessed noun, *var* (tur-03-09).
6. *Teyze-si*: aunt + 3SG possessive after a vowel, "his aunt" (tur-03-04). *On-u*: *o* + *n* + accusative. *O* never takes a case suffix directly (tur-03-10).
7. *Denize yakın*: *yakın* "near" takes the dative of what something is near (tur-03-02). *Evin büyük bir bahçesi var*: "the house's big garden exists", a part-whole "have" sentence; the adjective and *bir* stand before the possessed noun (tur-03-09).
8. *Bahçe-de-ki*: garden + locative + *-ki*, "the in-the-garden". A locative cannot describe a noun; *-ki* turns it into a describing word that stands before *ağaçlar* (tur-03-14).
9. *İstanbul'dan*: ablative, the starting point (tur-03-03). *İzmir'e*: dative, the goal (tur-03-02). *Otobüsle*: *-(y)lA*, the means of transport (tur-03-13). *Uçak bileti* is a *-(s)I* compound, "plane ticket", a kind of ticket (tur-03-08).
10. *Nasıl* is a question word, which never takes *mI* (tur-03-12). The second question is a yes-no question about a locative predicate, so it needs *mI*: *Ankara'da mı?* (tur-02-05).
11. *Kız kardeş-im-in*: the bare compound *kız kardeş* "sister", 1SG possessive, genitive: "of my sister" (tur-03-08, tur-03-05). *İş-i*: work + 3SG possessive, agreeing with the owner *kız kardeşimin* (tur-03-05).
12. *Teyze-si-yle*: aunt + 3SG possessive + *-(y)lA*, with *y* after the vowel and no *n*, because *-(y)lA* is not a case suffix (tur-03-13). *Merkez-i-ne*: centre + 3SG possessive + dative, with the *n* that every case takes after a 3rd-person possessive (tur-03-06). *Şehrin merkezi* is a genitive-possessive phrase, "the city's centre".
13. *İstanbul-da-ki-nden*: Istanbul + locative + *-ki* "the one in Istanbul" + ablative with *n*. The ablative here marks what something is compared with, "than the one in Istanbul" (tur-03-14, tur-03-03).
14. *El-in*: hand + genitive, the owner (tur-03-05). *Ses-i*: sound + 3SG possessive, the thing owned (tur-03-04).
15. *Bu çanta kimin? — Benim.* The genitive question word and the genitive pronoun as predicates (tur-03-12, tur-03-10).
16. *Fotoğraftaki adam dedem.* *Fotoğraf-ta-ki* describes *adam*; *dede-m* "my grandfather" is the predicate (tur-03-14, tur-03-04).
17. *Pasaportunuz var mı?* — *Nereye gidiyorsunuz?* The polite 2PL possessive with *var mı* (tur-03-09); *nereye* for movement to, and no *mI* (tur-03-12).
18. *Komşumun oğluyla konuşuyorum.* *Komşu-m-un* "of my neighbour" (genitive), *oğl-u-yla* "with his son": *oğul* loses its *u*, then the 3SG possessive, then *-(y)lA* with *y* (tur-03-01, tur-03-05, tur-03-13). *Konuşmak* takes *-(y)lA*.
19. *Hangi otobüs şehrin merkezine gidiyor?* *Hangi* before the noun; the goal in the dative with *n* after the possessive (tur-03-12, tur-03-06).
20. *Ali'nin kardeşi yok.* The thing owned must carry the possessive (tur-03-09).
21. *Arabasıyla İzmir'e gidiyor.* No *n* before *-(y)lA* (tur-03-13).
22. *Masadaki kitabı okuyorum.* A locative must take *-ki* to describe a noun (tur-03-14); the book is definite (tur-03-01).
23. *Kimi arıyorsun?* A question word takes no *mI*, and *kim* as object is always accusative (tur-03-12).
24. *Bana bir Türk kahvesi getiriyor.* The dative of *ben* is *bana* (tur-03-10); a nationality compound needs the marker (tur-03-08).
