## Before you start

This review covers the first half of part 4: the slot order of the verb (tur-04-01); commands with the bare stem, *-(y)In*, *-(y)InIz*, *-sIn* and *-sAnA* (tur-04-02); the past *-DI* and the k-set endings (tur-04-03); the future *-(y)AcAK* (tur-04-04); the aorist, its three shapes and the thirteen *-Ir* stems (tur-04-05); its meanings and the negative *-mAz* (tur-04-06); the evidential past *-mIş* (tur-04-07); and the copular markers *-(y)DI*, *-(y)mIş*, *-(y)sA* on nouns (tur-04-08). Everything from parts 2 and 3 is assumed: cases, possessives, the genitive, pronouns, *-(I)yor* and the negative *-mA*.

Two questions decide most answers in this review. Which slot does the suffix go in? And which person set does the suffix before it choose: the k-set after *-DI*, *-(y)DI* and *-(y)sA*, the z-set after the other tenses and *-(y)mIş*?

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread if you went wrong.

## Passage

**A. A message from İzmir.** A constructed passage, using only grammar taught so far. Ayşe writes to her brother Mehmet. Two words are glossed in place: *sürekli* "all the time" and *yolculuk* "journey" (from *yolcu* "traveller", tur-04-08).

1. Merhaba Mehmet! Dün akşam İzmir'e vardık.
2. Yolculuk çok uzundu ama eğlenceliydi. Otobüste herkes uyudu, ben uyumadım.
3. Teyzemin evi denize çok yakın. Bu sabah erken kalktım ve pencereden denize baktım.
4. Teyzem bana bir haber verdi: komşusu piyangoyu kazanmış!
5. Komşu şimdi çok zenginmiş, ama hâlâ eski evinde oturuyor ve sürekli çalışıyor.
6. Yarın birlikte müzeye gideceğiz. Sonra bir lokantada balık yiyeceğiz.
7. Teyzem her akşam bahçede çay içer ve kitap okur. Televizyon izlemez.
8. Sen ne yapıyorsun? Ankara'da hava nasıl? Burada hava çok sıcak.
9. Pazartesi döneceğim. Lütfen anneme söyle: merak etmesin!
10. Görüşürüz!

**B. At a café.** A constructed dialogue. Two words are glossed in place: *müşteri* "customer" and *başka* "other, else".

1. Garson: Buyurun, ne istersiniz?
2. Müşteri: Bir çay, lütfen. Şeker istemiyorum.
3. Garson: Hemen getiririm. Başka bir şey ister misiniz?
4. Müşteri: Hayır, teşekkür ederim.

## Exercises

1. Translate sentences 1 to 3 of passage A.
2. Translate sentences 4 and 5.
3. Translate sentences 6 and 7.
4. Translate sentences 8 to 10.
5. Translate passage B.
6. Make the customer's order in line 2 into a polite request: "Would you bring (me) a tea, please?"
7. In sentence 1, take apart *vardık*. Which slot does each piece fill, and why is the ending *-k* and not *-(y)Iz*?
8. In sentence 2, why does *uzundu* have no *y* while *eğlenceliydi* has one? What kind of marker is *-DI* here?
9. In sentence 4, why does Ayşe write *kazanmış* and not *kazandı*? And why *verdi*, not *vermiş*?
10. In sentence 5, take apart *zenginmiş*. Does it say the neighbour was rich, or is rich?
11. In sentence 7, explain the choice of *içer*, *okur* and *izlemez* rather than *-(I)yor* forms.
12. In sentence 9, take apart *merak etmesin*. Who is it addressed to, and who is meant to stop worrying?
13. In passage B, what does *getiririm* express, and why is *ister misiniz* not *istiyor musunuz*?
14. Name the slots filled in *yapmayacağız* and give its meaning.
15. Put *Ayşe evde* into the past, then the evidential, then "if Ayşe is at home" (preview of tur-06-10).
16. Build "Did you (one friend) understand?" and "Have they arrived (I hear)?"
17. Build "I'll call you tomorrow" as a promise and as a plan.
18. Give the aorist 3rd singular of *gelmek*, *gülmek*, *görmek*, *sormak*, *kalmak*, *kalkmak*.
19. Correct *\*Her sabah kahve içerim değil* ("I don't drink coffee in the mornings").
20. Correct *\*Dün akşam sinemaya gitmişim*, said by someone describing an evening he remembers well.
21. Correct *\*Yarın yağmur yağıyor*, said as a weather forecast.
22. Correct *\*Geldi misin?* and *\*Hastadım*.
23. Correct *\*Bekle!*, said to a waiter, and *\*Gelecekim*.

## Worked answers

1. "Hello Mehmet! We arrived in İzmir yesterday evening. The journey was very long but fun. Everyone slept on the bus; I didn't. My aunt's house is very near the sea. This morning I got up early and looked at the sea from the window." *Varmak* takes the dative of the goal (tur-04-05 vocabulary); *pencereden* "from the window" is ablative (tur-03-03); *uyumadım*: negative before the past (tur-04-03).
2. "My aunt gave me some news: her neighbour has won the lottery! The neighbour is apparently very rich now, but he still lives in his old house and works all the time." The win is reported in *-mIş* (tur-04-07); *oturuyor*, *çalışıyor* report what the aunt sees (tur-02-08).
3. "Tomorrow we're going to the museum together. Then we'll eat fish at a restaurant. My aunt drinks tea in the garden and reads every evening. She doesn't watch television." Plans in the future (tur-04-04); habits in the aorist (tur-04-06).
4. "What are you doing? How's the weather in Ankara? Here it's very hot. I'll be back on Monday. Please tell Mum: she's not to worry! See you!" *Pazartesi döneceğim* is a plan (tur-04-04); *söyle* is a command to one friend (tur-04-02); *görüşürüz* is a set aorist phrase (tur-04-05).
5. Waiter: "Yes, please, what would you like?" Customer: "A tea, please. I don't want sugar." Waiter: "I'll bring it right away. Would you like anything else?" Customer: "No, thank you." *Buyurun* (tur-04-02), *istersiniz* and *ister misiniz* (aorist offers, tur-04-06), *teşekkür ederim* (tur-04-05).
6. *Bana bir çay getirir misiniz, lütfen?* A request is a 2nd-person aorist question; *getir-* has two syllables, so *-ir*; *mI* takes the polite ending *-siniz* (tur-04-05, tur-04-06).
7. *Var-dı-k*: stem (slot 1), past *-DI* (slot 5), person (slot 7). *D* is *d* after the voiced *r*; *ı* by harmony. After *-DI*, the person endings come from the k-set, where "we" is *-k* (tur-04-01, tur-04-03).
8. *Uzun-du*: after a consonant, no *y*. *Eğlenceli-ydi*: after a vowel, the copula keeps its *y*, the remnant of the old verb *i-* (tur-04-08). In both, *-(y)DI* is the copular marker on an adjective (slot 6), not the verbal past *-DI*.
9. Ayşe did not see the neighbour win; she heard about it. Reported events take *-mIş* (tur-04-07). The aunt's giving the news is something Ayşe witnessed herself, so *verdi*.
10. *Zengin-miş*: adjective + evidential copula, no tense of its own (tur-04-08). With *şimdi* "now", it means "is rich (I'm told)".
11. They describe the aunt's lasting habits, her character: the aorist states what she does as a rule (tur-04-06). *İzlemez* is the negative aorist, *-mAz*. *Çay içiyor* would report what Ayşe happens to see during her visit.
12. *Merak et-me-sin*: *merak etmek* "worry", negative *-mA*, 3rd-person command *-sIn*: "let her not worry". Mehmet is asked to pass the message on; the mother is the one who should not worry (tur-04-02).
13. A promise made as he speaks: "I'll bring it right away" (tur-04-06). An offer is a 2nd-person aorist question; *istiyor musunuz* would ask about a present wish, not make an offer (tur-04-06).
14. *Yap-ma-yacağ-ız*: stem (1), negative (3), future (5), person (7): "we will not do (it)". *-mA* is written *ma*; *k* becomes *ğ* before the vowel of *-ız* (tur-04-01, tur-04-04).
15. *Ayşe evdeydi* "Ayşe was at home"; *Ayşe evdeymiş* "Ayşe is (or was) at home, apparently"; *Ayşe evdeyse* "if Ayşe is at home". After the vowel of *evde*, each marker keeps its *y* (tur-04-08).
16. *Anladın mı?*: past, k-set *-n*, then *mI* after the whole verb (tur-04-03). *Gelmişler mi?*: *-mIş*, "they" *-ler*, then *mi* (tur-04-07).
17. Promise: *Yarın seni ararım* (aorist, tur-04-06). Plan: *Yarın seni arayacağım* (future, tur-04-04). *Ara-* ends in a vowel: *-r*, and *-yacak* with *y*.
18. *Gelir* (one of the thirteen), *güler* (not on the list, though it ends in *l*), *görür* (on the list), *sorar* (not on the list), *kalır* (on the list), *kalkar* (one syllable, not on the list) (tur-04-05).
19. *Her sabah kahve içmem.* Verbs are negated inside, and the negative aorist of the 1st singular is *-mA-m* with no *z* (tur-04-06).
20. *Dün akşam sinemaya gittim.* What you did and remember takes *-DI*; *-mIş* would mean you had no memory of it (tur-04-07).
21. *Yarın yağmur yağacak.* A forecast is a prediction: *-(y)AcAK*. *-(I)yor* for the future presents a fixed arrangement (tur-04-04).
22. *Geldin mi?*: after *-DI* the person ending stays on the verb (tur-04-03). *Hastaydım*: after a vowel, the copula has *y* (tur-04-08).
23. *Bekleyin* or *Lütfen bekleyin*: the bare stem is for people you call *sen* (tur-04-02). *Geleceğim*: before a vowel, *k* becomes *ğ* (tur-04-04).
