## Before you start

This review covers all of part 4, with the weight on its second half: the slot order (tur-04-01); commands (tur-04-02); *-DI* (tur-04-03); the future (tur-04-04); the aorist and *-mAz* (tur-04-05, tur-04-06); *-mIş* (tur-04-07); the copular markers (tur-04-08); compound tenses (tur-04-10); *-DIr* (tur-04-11); *-mAlI* (tur-04-12); *-(y)Abil* and *-(y)AmA* (tur-04-13); the optative (tur-04-14); the place of *mI* (tur-04-15); *olmak* (tur-04-16); and the choice of tense (tur-04-17). Parts 2 and 3 are assumed.

For every verb, ask the questions of tur-04-17: when, seen how, known how? Then build it slot by slot.

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread if you went wrong.

## Passage

**A. Öküzün suçu, "The ox's fault".** An attested Nasreddin Hoca anecdote (trwikisource, *Öküzün suçu*), given here in part; the omitted sentences use forms from part 6 and are summarised in square brackets. Words glossed in place: *tarla* "field", *öküz* "ox", *ortalık* "the surroundings, everything around", *dağıtmak* "scatter, mess up", *aradan* "in between", *suç* "fault, crime", *hayvan* "animal", *karışmak* "interfere".

1. Bir gün Nasreddin Hoca'nın tarlasına bir öküz girmiş.
2. Ortalığı dağıtmış.
3. [The Hoca takes a stick and chases the animal, but it escapes.]
4. Aradan günler geçmiş.
5. [On his way to the market, the Hoca sees the same ox pulling a cart, and starts scolding it with his stick. The owner, a villager, is astonished, and asks:]
6. – Hoca, Hoca, ne yapıyorsun? Suçu ne bu hayvanın?
7. Nasreddin Hoca: – Sen karışma! [It knows very well what its fault is!]

**B. Two friends.** A constructed dialogue. Words glossed in place: *de* "too, also" (taught in part 5), *akşamları* "in the evenings" (tur-05-04), *buluşmak* "meet up".

1. Ali: Dün akşam neredeydin? Seni aradım ama telefonu açmadın.
2. Ayşe: Özür dilerim, sinemadaydım. Telefonum kapalıydı.
3. Ali: Film nasıldı?
4. Ayşe: Çok güzeldi. Sen de gelmeliydin!
5. Ali: Gelemezdim, çok işim vardı. Bu akşam ne yapıyorsun?
6. Ayşe: Henüz bilmiyorum. Belki Mehmet'le yemek yiyeceğiz. Sen de gelir misin?
7. Ali: Olur, gelirim. Mehmet yeni bir işe başlamış, değil mi?
8. Ayşe: Evet, bir bankada çalışıyormuş. Çok mutluymuş.
9. Ali: Herhalde akşamları çok yorgundur. Erken bitirelim o zaman.
10. Ayşe: Tamam. Akşam lokantada buluşalım.

## Exercises

1. Translate passage A, lines 1, 2 and 4.
2. Translate passage A, lines 6 and 7.
3. Why is the whole narrative of passage A in *-mIş*, while the dialogue is not?
4. In line 6 of passage A, take apart *yapıyorsun* and *suçu*. What is unusual about the word order of *Suçu ne bu hayvanın?*
5. In line 7, take apart *karışma*. What would the polite form be?
6. Translate passage B, lines 1 to 5.
7. Translate passage B, lines 6 to 10.
8. In B1 and B2, explain *neredeydin*, *açmadın* and *kapalıydı*.
9. In B4 and B5, explain *gelmeliydin* and *gelemezdim*. What do the two have in common?
10. In B6, why *yiyeceğiz* and not *yiyoruz*? Why is *gelir misin* an invitation?
11. In B7, why does Ali say *başlamış*, and what kind of question is *değil mi*?
12. In B8, take apart *çalışıyormuş* and *mutluymuş*, and say what each slot contributes.
13. In B9, what does *yorgundur* express? Take apart *bitirelim*.
14. Name the slots in *bekleyemeyecektik* and translate it.
15. Put *Ali evde* into the future, into "must be", and into "can't be".
16. Build "I've been learning Turkish for two years, but I still can't speak (it)."
17. Build "When we were children, we used to swim in the sea every summer" (use *Çocukken* "when we were children", a preview).
18. Ask a stranger politely: "Could you tell me the way to the station?" (*istasyonun yolunu* "the way to the station").
19. Say "Let's not be late!" and "Shall I call a taxi?"
20. Is *Dün akşam yağmur yağıyordu; sonra durdu mu?* correct? Explain each verb form.
21. Correct *\*Ali doktordu geçen yıl* ("Ali became a doctor last year") and *\*Yarın evdeyeceğim*.
22. Correct *\*Geldi misiniz?* and *\*Gelecek misinizler?*
23. Correct *\*Okuyabılırım* and *\*Ben yüzebilmem* ("I can't swim").
24. Correct *\*Telefonu çaldı, ben duş alırdım* ("The phone rang while I was having a shower").
25. Correct *\*Komşumuz piyangoyu kazandı*, said by someone who read it in the paper, and *\*Ben öğretmenimdir*, said by someone introducing himself.

## Worked answers

1. "One day an ox got into Nasreddin Hoca's field. It made a mess of everything. Days went by." *Tarla-sı-na*: field + 3SG possessive + dative with *n* (tur-03-06); *gir-miş*, *dağıt-mış*, *geç-miş*: the narrative *-mIş* (tur-04-07).
2. "'Hoca, Hoca, what are you doing? What is this animal's fault?' Nasreddin Hoca: 'Don't you interfere!'"
3. The story is told, not witnessed by the teller, so its events take *-mIş*. Inside the dialogue the characters speak of what they see and know at first hand: *yapıyorsun* reports what the villager sees, *karışma* is a command (tur-04-07, tur-04-17).
4. *Yap-ıyor-sun*: do, progressive, 2nd singular. *Suç-u*: fault + 3SG possessive. The genitive possessor *bu hayvanın* comes after the predicate *ne*, not before *suçu* as usual: an afterthought placed at the end, a word-order freedom of speech (tur-02-07, tur-03-05).
5. *Karış-ma*: interfere + negative, the bare command to *sen*. Polite: *Karışmayın* (tur-04-02).
6. "Ali: Where were you yesterday evening? I rang you but you didn't answer the phone. Ayşe: Sorry, I was at the cinema. My phone was off. Ali: How was the film? Ayşe: Very good. You should have come too! Ali: I couldn't have come, I had a lot of work. What are you doing this evening?"
7. "Ayşe: I don't know yet. Maybe we'll have dinner with Mehmet. Will you come too? Ali: OK, I'll come. Mehmet has started a new job, hasn't he? Ayşe: Yes, he's working at a bank, apparently. He's very happy, it seems. Ali: He'll probably be very tired in the evenings. Let's finish early, then. Ayşe: All right. Let's meet at the restaurant in the evening."
8. *Nerede-ydi-n*: where + past copula with *y* after a vowel + k-set "you" (tur-04-08). *Aç-ma-dı-n*: negative before *-DI*, k-set (tur-04-03). *Kapalı-ydı*: past copula on an adjective (tur-04-08).
9. *Gel-meli-ydi-n*: necessitative + past copula: an obligation not met (tur-04-12). *Gel-eme-z-di-m*: impotential + aorist *z* + past copula: "I couldn't have, wouldn't have been able to" (tur-04-13, tur-04-10). Both are contrary to fact: neither person came.
10. *Yiyeceğiz* gives a possibility not yet fixed, with *belki*; *yiyoruz* would present it as an arrangement (tur-04-04). *Gelir misin?* is a 2nd-person aorist question about willingness, which makes it an offer (tur-04-06).
11. Ali heard it from someone: *-mIş* (tur-04-07). *Değil mi* is a tag asking Ayşe to confirm (tur-04-15).
12. *Çalış-ıyor-muş*: work, ongoing (slot 5), evidential copula (slot 6): hearsay about something going on (tur-04-10). *Mutlu-ymuş*: adjective + evidential copula, with *y* after the vowel: "he is happy, they say" (tur-04-08).
13. An assumption: "he must be, he'll be tired" (tur-04-11). *Bitir-elim*: finish + optative 1st plural: "let's finish" (tur-04-14).
14. *Bekle-yeme-yecek-ti-k*: wait (1), cannot (4), future (5), past copula (6), "we" (7): "we weren't going to be able to wait" (tur-04-01, tur-04-13, tur-04-10).
15. *Ali evde olacak* (future needs *olmak*). *Ali evde olmalı* (deduction). *Ali evde olamaz* ("impossible") (tur-04-16).
16. *İki yıldır Türkçe öğreniyorum ama hâlâ konuşamıyorum.* *-DIr* on the length of time with *-(I)yor* (tur-04-11); *konuş-amı-yor-um*, inability in the present (tur-04-13).
17. *Çocukken her yaz denizde yüzerdik.* A characteristic past habit: aorist + past copula (tur-04-10). *-ken* "when, while" is taught in part 6.
18. *Bana istasyonun yolunu söyleyebilir misiniz?* A tentative request with *-(y)Abil* + aorist question (tur-04-13).
19. *Geç kalmayalım!* (negative optative, tur-04-14). *Taksi çağırayım mı?* (consultative optative, *mI* after the ending, tur-04-15).
20. The sentence is correct. *Yağıyordu*: rain in progress at a past time (tur-04-10); *durdu*: the event that ended it, *-DI*; *mu* after the 3rd singular *-DI* form, a real question about whether it stopped (tur-04-15). The trap was the expectation of an error: not every sentence in a review is wrong.
21. *Ali geçen yıl doktor oldu*: "became" needs *olmak*; the copula *-(y)DI* is "was" (tur-04-16). *Yarın evde olacağım*: the future of a noun predicate needs *olmak* (tur-04-16).
22. *Geldiniz mi?*: after *-DI* the person ending stays on the verb (tur-04-15). *Gelecek misiniz?*: after the future the ending moves onto *mI*, and there is only one 2nd plural ending (tur-04-15).
23. *Okuyabilirim*: *bil* never harmonises. *Ben yüzemem*: "cannot" is *-(y)AmA*, with no *z* in the 1st singular (tur-04-13).
24. *Telefon çaldı, ben duş alıyordum*: the background in progress is *-(I)yordu*; the aorist never presents an action as going on (tur-04-10). *Telefon* is the subject, so no accusative.
25. *Komşumuz piyangoyu kazanmış*: news from a newspaper takes *-mIş* (tur-04-07). *Ben öğretmenim*: *-DIr* with the 1st person turns the statement into a guess (tur-04-11).
