## The idea

The postpositions of tur-05-05 follow a bare noun. Most others require a case on the noun before them, and the case is not arbitrary. It is the case whose own meaning fits (goksel2005 §§17.2.2, 17.2.3).

- **The dative -(y)A means "to, towards"** (tur-03-02). So the postpositions that point at something take it: *denize doğru* "towards the sea", *akşama kadar* "until the evening" (up to the evening), *sana karşı* "against you" (facing you), *bana göre* "in my view" (as it looks to me).
- **The ablative -DAn means "from"** (tur-03-03). So the postpositions that measure from a starting point take it: *yemekten sonra* "after the meal" (later, from the meal), *yemekten önce* "before the meal", *pazartesiden beri* "since Monday", *bundan başka* "apart from this".

So once you know what a postposition means, you can usually guess its case. The table at the end is the reference.

You already know *önce* "first; ago" and *sonra* "then; later" as adverbs (tur-04-03), and *kadar* with a bare noun (tur-05-05). With a case before them they become postpositions, and their meaning shifts with the case: *Ali kadar* "as much as Ali", but *Ali'ye kadar* "as far as Ali, up to Ali".

**The eight pronouns are not special here.** With these postpositions, pronouns take the case like any noun: *bana göre*, *senden önce*, *ondan sonra* (goksel2005 §17.2.2, ex. 33; §17.2.3, ex. 48).

## How it is built

1. **Decide the relation**: "after the lesson".
2. **Pick the postposition**: *sonra* "after".
3. **Put the noun in the case it requires**: *sonra* takes the ablative: *ders-ten*. (*Ders* ends in voiceless *s*: *-ten*.)
4. **Add the postposition**: *dersten sonra*.
5. **Modify it, if you need to.** *Önce* and *sonra* alone can take an amount before them: *dersten biraz sonra* "a little after the lesson", *dersten beş dakika önce* "five minutes before the lesson" (goksel2005 §17.2.3).
6. **Place the phrase** where an adverb goes, usually before the verb or at the start: *Dersten sonra kahve içtik.*

**With the dative.**

- ***göre*** "according to; compared with; suitable for": *hava durumuna göre* "according to the forecast", *ötekine göre* "compared with the other one", *sana göre* "right for you; in your opinion" (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (ii)).
- ***doğru*** "towards", in space or time: *denize doğru*, *akşama doğru* "towards evening".
- ***kadar*** "until, as far as, by": *saat bire kadar* "until one o'clock", *cumaya kadar* "by Friday", *Van'a kadar* "as far as Van" (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (iii)).
- ***karşı*** "against; towards (an attitude); towards (a time)": *sana karşı*, *babama karşı saygı* "respect for my father", *sabaha karşı* "towards morning" (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (iv)).
- ***rağmen*** "in spite of": *yağmura rağmen* "in spite of the rain" (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (v)).

**With the ablative.**

- ***önce*** "before" and ***sonra*** "after": *benden önce*, *yemekten sonra*.
- ***beri*** "since; for (and still)": *pazartesiden beri* "since Monday", *üç günden beri* "for three days" (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (ii)). The verb is in a form that shows the situation still going on, as with *-DIr* (tur-05-04).
- ***başka*** "apart from, other than": *bundan başka* "apart from this" (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (i)).
- ***dolayı*** "because of": *kardan dolayı* "because of the snow" (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (iv)).
- ***itibaren*** "from, as of", for a planned start: *pazartesiden itibaren* "from Monday on" (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (v)).

## Where it applies

- **Göre**: according to a source (*hava durumuna göre*); someone's opinion (*bana göre*, close to *bence*, tur-04-12); comparison (*ötekine göre*); suitability (*Bu iş sana göre* "This job is right for you") (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (ii)).
- **Doğru**: direction in space (*denize doğru*) or approach in time (*akşama doğru*).
- **Kadar with the dative**: until a time (*beşe kadar*), by a deadline (*Bu iş cumaya kadar biter* "This work will be done by Friday"), within (*beş dakikaya kadar*), as far as a place (*Van'a kadar*) (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (iii)).
- **Karşı**: opposition (*bana karşı*), an attitude to someone (*babama karşı saygım var*), towards a time (*sabaha karşı*). As a noun, *karşı* is "the opposite side": *karşıya geçmek* "cross to the other side" (wiktionary).
- **Rağmen**: in spite of a noun (*yağmura rağmen*); in spite of a clause, *-mAsInA rağmen* (tur-06-14).
- **Önce, sonra with the ablative**: before or after an event, a time or a person: *yemekten sonra*, *saat beşten önce*, *benden önce*; with an amount: *bir saat sonra*. Without the ablative they are adverbs: *iki gün önce* "two days ago" (tur-04-03). Before and after clauses: *-mAdAn önce*, *-DIktAn sonra* (tur-06-07).
- **Beri**: since a point (*pazartesiden beri*, *1995'ten beri*) or for a length (*üç günden beri*), with a present or ongoing predicate; with a negative or counted predicate, *-DI* or *-mIş* is common (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (ii)): *O zamandan beri onu görmedim* "I haven't seen him since then".
- **Başka**: "apart from" with the ablative; without it, the adjective "other" (tur-05-02).
- **Dolayı**: cause; its synonyms are *ötürü*, less frequent, and *yüzünden* (tur-05-07) (goksel2005 §§16.4.4, 17.2.3 (iv)).
- **İtibaren**: start of a planned state or period.
- **Describing a noun**: *sonra* and *önce* have *-ki* forms, *sonraki* and *önceki* (tur-03-14), which can follow an ablative: *yemekten sonraki ders* "the lesson after the meal" (goksel2005 §15.3.7).

## Watch out

**Do not use the bare noun with these.** *\*Yemek sonra*, *\*pazartesi beri*, *\*ben göre* copy the bare pattern of *için*. Each needs its case: *yemekten sonra*, *pazartesiden beri*, *bana göre*.

**Do not put the eight pronouns in the genitive.** *\*Benim göre* and *\*onun sonra* carry over the rule of tur-05-05. Here the case is the dative or ablative for every noun and pronoun: *bana göre*, *ondan sonra*.

**Do not swap the cases.** *\*Yemeğe sonra* and *\*denizden doğru* reverse the logic: "after" measures from a starting point (ablative), "towards" points at a goal (dative).

**Do not confuse the two kadar's.** *Ali kadar* (bare) is "as much as Ali"; *Ali'ye kadar* (dative) is "up to Ali". *\*Beş kadar bekledim* means "I waited about five" (tur-05-05). "I waited until five" is *Beşe kadar bekledim*.

**Do not use the past with beri for a state that still holds.** English "I have been ill since Tuesday" tempts *\*Salıdan beri hasta oldum*. The state continues: *Salıdan beri hastayım* (goksel2005 §17.2.3 (ii)).

**Do not use beri for a future start.** *\*Pazartesiden beri başlıyorum* is wrong for "starting from Monday": *beri* looks back from now. A planned start is *itibaren*: *Pazartesiden itibaren*.

**Do not put the amount after önce or sonra.** *\*Benden önce beş dakika* follows English "five minutes before me" in the wrong place. The amount stands between the ablative and the postposition: *benden beş dakika önce*.

**Do not use göre for "compared with" and also daha with the ablative.** *\*Ötekinden göre* mixes two patterns. Either *ötekinden daha ucuz* (tur-05-02) or *ötekine göre ucuz*.

**Do not read doğru here as "right".** *Doğru* "true, correct" (tur-01-03) after a dative is "towards": *eve doğru* "towards the house", not "right for the house".

## Connections

Japanese and Korean also mark these relations after the noun, with a case particle followed by a word: Korean 밥 먹은 후에 "after eating" and Japanese 食事の後で "after the meal" put "after" last, as *yemekten sonra* does. Turkish differs in making the case before *sonra* the ablative, "from the meal, later". Spanish *después de la comida* and French *après le repas* have the opposite order.

## Exercises

1. Say "before the film", "after the film" and "since the film".
2. Translate *Bana göre en iyi lokanta bu.*
3. A learner writes *\*Ders sonra parka gittik.* Correct it.
4. Build "I'll wait until six o'clock."
5. Translate *Yağmura rağmen yürüyüşe devam ettik.*
6. Say "I've been living in İzmir since 2020" and "I've been living in İzmir for six years", once with *beri* and once with *-DIr*.
7. Why is *\*Senin göre* wrong? Give the right form.
8. Translate *Akşama doğru fırtına başladı ve sabaha karşı bitti.*
9. Say "Apart from Ali, everyone came to the meeting."
10. Correct *\*Haftaya beri yeni bir kursa katılıyorum* meant as "From next week I'm joining a new course" (*kurs* "course").

## Worked answers

1. *Filmden önce*, *filmden sonra*, *filmden beri*. All three take the ablative (goksel2005 §17.2.3).
2. "In my opinion this is the best restaurant." *Bana göre*: dative + *göre*; *en iyi* (tur-05-02).
3. *Dersten sonra parka gittik.* *Sonra* as "after" needs the ablative; *s* is voiceless, so *-ten*.
4. *Saat altıya kadar beklerim* (a promise, aorist) or *bekleyeceğim*. *Altı* + dative *-ya* + *kadar*.
5. "In spite of the rain we carried on with the walk." *Devam etmek* takes the dative: *yürüyüşe*.
6. *2020'den beri İzmir'de oturuyorum.* *Altı yıldan beri İzmir'de oturuyorum* or *Altı yıldır İzmir'de oturuyorum* (tur-05-04). The present, because it still holds.
7. *Göre* takes the dative for every noun and pronoun; the genitive rule belongs to *için*, *gibi*, *kadar*, *ile*. *Sana göre.*
8. "Towards evening the storm began, and towards morning it ended." *Doğru* and *karşı* with the dative both mean "towards" a time; *sabaha karşı* is a fixed use (goksel2005 §17.2.2 (iv)).
9. *Ali'den başka herkes toplantıya geldi.* *Başka* with the ablative: "other than Ali".
10. *Haftaya* is already "next week"; for a planned start use *itibaren* with an ablative: *Gelecek haftadan itibaren yeni bir kursa katılıyorum.* *Beri* looks back from now and cannot mark a future start.
