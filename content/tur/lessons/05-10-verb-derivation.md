## The idea

Tur-05-09 made nouns and adjectives from nouns. This lesson crosses the line between word classes: nouns and adjectives become verbs, and verbs become nouns.

**Noun or adjective to verb.** Three suffixes, built on one another, do most of the work (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1):

- ***-lA*** makes a verb that does or puts the thing: *tuz* "salt" → *tuzla-* "salt (something)"; *temiz* "clean" → *temizle-* "clean (something)", which you know from tur-04-01. With some adjectives the verb means "become": *zayıf* "thin" → *zayıfla-* "get thin".
- ***-lAş*** is *-lA* plus the reciprocal *-(I)ş* (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1). It means "become" with adjectives: *güzel* → *güzelleş-* "become beautiful", *kolay* → *kolaylaş-* "become easy"; and "do to each other" with nouns: *mektup* "letter" → *mektuplaş-* "write to each other".
- ***-lAn*** is *-lA* plus the passive or reflexive *-n*. It makes verbs that happen to oneself, with no object: *güneş* "sun" → *güneşlen-* "sunbathe", *hazır* "ready" → *hazırlan-* "get ready" (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1).

So *-lA* is the base, and the extra *ş* or *n* adds "each other" or "oneself". You will meet the reciprocal and reflexive as live suffixes in part 7 (tur-07-03); here they are frozen inside the derived verbs.

**Verb to noun.** Two suffixes name the action or its result:

- ***-(y)Iş*** "the act or way of doing": *gir-* "enter" → *giriş* "entrance", *çık-* "go out" → *çıkış* "exit", *yürü-* "walk" → *yürüyüş* "a walk" (goksel2005 §7.2.1.1). You know *giriş*, *kalkış* and *yürüyüş* already.
- ***-mA***, in some words, a thing produced by the action: *dondur-* "freeze (something)" → *dondurma* "ice cream" (goksel2005 §7.2.1.1). The same *-mA* is also the verbal noun of part 6 (tur-06-02), which can be made from any verb; here it is a word in the dictionary.

## How it is built

**A new verb.**

1. **Choose the root**: *kirli* "dirty".
2. **Choose the suffix by meaning**: "make dirty" is causative; Göksel and Kerslake list *kirlet-* under *-lAt* (= *-lA* + causative *-t*) (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1). The verb contains *kir* "dirt" (tur-01-01), not the whole of *kirli*.
3. **Harmonise**: *-lA* and *-lAş*, *-lAn* have two-way harmony: *tuz-la*, *temiz-le*, *güzel-leş*, *kolay-laş*, *güneş-len*.
4. **Make the infinitive and the aorist**: *-lA* verbs end in a vowel, so their aorist is *-r* (tur-04-05): *tuzlar*, *temizler*, *zayıflar*. *-lAş* and *-lAn* verbs have more than one syllable and end in a consonant, so their aorist is *-Ir*: *güzelleşir*, *kolaylaşır*, *güneşlenir* (wiktionary).
5. **Inflect it like any verb**: *Hava serinledi* "It has got cooler".

**A new noun.**

1. **Take the verb stem**: *bak-* "look".
2. **Add -(y)Iş**, with four-way harmony and *y* after a vowel: *bak-ış* "a look, a glance", *sat-ış* "sale", *anla-y-ış* "understanding", *yürü-y-üş*.
3. **Inflect it like a noun**: *çıkışta* "at the exit", *girişi* "its entrance".

## Where it applies

**-lA**

- putting or using a thing: *tuzla-* "salt", *fırçala-* "brush" (tur-04-12, from *fırça*);
- making a quality: *temizle-* "clean", from *temiz*;
- becoming a quality, intransitive: *zayıfla-* "get thin", *serinle-* "get cooler" (wiktionary);
- sounds: *havla-* "bark" (tur-04-06) from an imitation of the sound (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1).

**-lAş**

- becoming: *güzelleş-*, *kolaylaş-*;
- mutual action: *mektuplaş-* "write to each other", *haberleş-* "keep in touch".

**-lAn**

- action on or for oneself: *güneşlen-*, *hazırlan-*;
- becoming: *yaşlan-* "grow old" from *yaş* "age" (wiktionary); *evlen-* "get married" is built from *ev* "house" (wiktionary; tur-03-13).

**-lAt**: making something have a quality: *kirlet-* "make dirty" (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1).

**-(y)Iş**: the act, a single occurrence, the place or the manner: *giriş*, *çıkış*, *yürüyüş*, *bakış* "glance", *satış* "sale", *alış* "buying", *anlayış* "understanding", *uçuş* "flight" (from *uç-* "fly"), *kalkış* "departure" (tur-04-17).

**-mA as a word**: *dondurma*, *okuma* "reading (as a subject)". The productive verbal noun *-mA* with a possessive is tur-06-02.

## Watch out

**Do not use -lA for "become" by default.** Some adjectives take *-lA* (*zayıfla-*), some *-lAş* (*güzelleş-*), some *-lAn* (*hazırlan-* "get ready"). The choice is a fact of each word; Göksel and Kerslake give no rule that predicts it. Learn each verb as you meet it, and do not build *\*güzelle-* for "become beautiful". *Kolaylan-* exists, but TDK gives it as "be nearing completion", not "become easy".

**Do not give a -lAn verb an object.** *-lAn* verbs are intransitive (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1). *\*Kahvaltıyı hazırlandım* is wrong; "I prepared breakfast" is *Kahvaltıyı hazırladım*. *Hazırlandım* alone is "I got ready".

**Do not use the -Ar aorist on -lAş and -lAn verbs.** They are polysyllabic, so the aorist is *-Ir*: *güzelleşir*, *güneşlenir*, not *\*güzelleşer* (tur-04-05).

**Do not confuse a derived -mA noun with the verbal noun.** *Dondurma* is "ice cream", a fixed word. The verbal noun of part 6, *dondurması* "his freezing (of it)", is made from any verb and needs a possessive.

**Do not use -(y)Iş for every "-ing".** English "reading", "swimming" as activities are usually *-mAk* or *-mA* forms (part 6); *-(y)Iş* gives particular nouns: *yürüyüş* "a walk", not "walking" in general.

**Do not forget the buffer y.** *\*Yürüüş*, *\*anlaış*: after a vowel, *-(y)Iş* begins with *y*: *yürüyüş*, *anlayış*.

**Do not reach for etmek when a -lA verb exists.** *Temiz etmek* is not the verb for "clean"; *temizlemek* is. *Etmek* goes with nouns that have no verb of their own (tur-03-10): *yardım etmek*, *telefon etmek*.

## Connections

Japanese and Korean make verbs from nouns mainly with "do": Japanese 掃除する, Korean 청소하다 "clean". Turkish has the same strategy in *etmek* (tur-03-10) but also these suffixes, which have no close match there. Spanish *-ear* and French *-iser* make verbs from nouns and adjectives (*salar* "salt" from *sal*), and Spanish *-se* verbs (*prepararse* "get ready") match *-lAn* in meaning: *hazırlan-*.

## Exercises

1. Make verbs from *tuz*, *temiz* and *güneş*, and give each aorist.
2. Translate *Hava serinledi, ceketini al.*
3. Take apart *güzelleşti* and *kolaylaşıyor*.
4. A learner writes *\*Ben yemeği hazırlandım.* Correct it and explain.
5. Say "We keep in touch (with each other)" and "I write letters to Ayşe; we write to each other."
6. Translate *Uçuş saat kaçta? Girişte bekliyorum.*
7. Why is it *güzelleşir* and not *\*güzelleşer*?
8. Translate *Dedem çok yaşlandı ama hâlâ her gün yürüyüş yapıyor.*
9. Correct *\*Odayı temiz ettim.*

## Worked answers

1. *Tuzlamak* (aor. *tuzlar*), *temizlemek* (*temizler*), *güneşlenmek* (*güneşlenir*). *-lA* verbs end in a vowel: aorist *-r*; *-lAn* verbs are polysyllabic and consonant-final: *-Ir* (tur-04-05).
2. "It has got cooler; take your jacket." *Serin-le-di*: a change of state in the past for the present state (tur-04-03).
3. *Güzel-leş-ti*: adjective, "become", past. *Kolay-laş-ıyor*: adjective, "become", progressive. Harmony: *e* after *e*, *a* after *a*.
4. *Ben yemeği hazırladım.* *Hazırlan-* is intransitive, "get oneself ready"; with an object you need *hazırla-* (goksel2005 §7.2.2.1).
5. *Haberleşiyoruz.* *Ayşe'yle mektuplaşıyorum.* The partner of a mutual action takes *-(y)lA*.
6. "What time is the flight? I'm waiting at the entrance." *Uç-uş*, *gir-iş-te*: *-(y)Iş* nouns, the second with the locative.
7. *Güzelleş-* has three syllables and ends in a consonant; such stems take *-Ir* in the aorist (tur-04-05).
8. "My grandfather has grown very old, but he still goes for a walk every day." *Yaş-lan-dı*; *yürüyüş yapmak*; *hâlâ* "still" (tur-01-03).
9. *Odayı temizledim.* The verb *temizle-* exists; *etmek* is for nouns with no verb of their own.
