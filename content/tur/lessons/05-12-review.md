## Before you start

This review covers all of part 5: adjectives and adverbs (tur-05-01); comparison with *daha* and *en* (tur-05-02); numbers, ordinals and distributives (tur-05-03); time, days and dates (tur-05-04); postpositions with the bare noun (tur-05-05) and with the dative and ablative (tur-05-06); location nouns (tur-05-07); conjunctions and *de* (tur-05-08); derivation (tur-05-09, tur-05-10); and reduplication (tur-05-11). Parts 2 to 4 are assumed: the verb forms of part 4 appear throughout.

For every phrase, ask: what is the head, and what case or postposition links the other words to it? For every derived word, take it apart from the root outwards.

Work each exercise before reading its answer. Every answer names the lesson to reread if you went wrong.

## Passage

**A. A postcard from İzmir.** A constructed text. Words glossed in place: *tam* "right, exactly", *görünmek* "be seen, look", *levrek* "sea bass", *sevgiler* "love (at the end of a letter)".

1. Sevgili Ali,
2. İzmir'e geçen cuma geldik. Otelimiz denizin tam karşısında.
3. Odamız küçük ama tertemiz. Pencereden deniz masmavi görünüyor.
4. Sabahları saat dokuzda kahvaltı ediyoruz, sonra da sahilde uzun bir yürüyüş yapıyoruz.
5. Burada her şey İstanbul'dan daha ucuz, hava da daha sıcak.
6. Dün öğleden sonra iki saat güneşlendik.
7. Mehmet benden daha çok yüzüyor. O hem denizi hem de balığı çok seviyor.
8. Otelin yanında küçük bir lokanta var. Çeşit çeşit balık var, ama biz en çok levreği beğendik.
9. Yemekten sonra herkese birer dondurma aldım.
10. 25 Ağustos'a kadar buradayız.
11. Seni çok özledik! Ne telefon ediyorsun ne de mesaj gönderiyorsun.
12. Sevgiler, Ayşe

## Exercises

1. Translate passage A, lines 1 to 4.
2. Translate passage A, lines 5 to 8.
3. Translate passage A, lines 9 to 12.
4. In line 2, take apart *karşısında* and explain why *denizin* is in the genitive.
5. In line 3, what kind of word is *tertemiz*, and why could the writer not say *\*çok tertemiz*? What is *masmavi* doing before *görünüyor*?
6. In line 4, explain *sabahları*, *saat dokuzda* and the *de* in *sonra da*.
7. In line 5, explain *İstanbul'dan* and the *de* in *hava da*. Could *daha* be left out?
8. In line 6, why does *iki saat* have no case? Take apart *güneşlendik*.
9. In line 7, take apart *benden daha çok* and explain *hem ... hem de*.
10. In line 8, explain *otelin yanında*, *çeşit çeşit* and *en çok*.
11. In line 9, why *birer*? Could Ayşe write *\*herkese birer dondurmalar*?
12. In line 10, read the date aloud in words and explain *kadar*.
13. In line 11, what does *ne ... ne de* do to the verbs, which are positive?
14. Translate proverb B, and explain *gümüşse* and *altındır*.
15. Say "My brother is two years older than me, but I am taller" (*yaş* "year of age").
16. Build "the three most expensive hotels in Ankara".
17. Say "The meeting starts at a quarter to ten and goes on until noon."
18. Build "I've been living in this neighbourhood since 2015."
19. Say "There is a big tree behind our house, and under the tree there is a bench" (*bank* "bench").
20. Make adjectives meaning "unemployed" and "with a sea view" (*deniz manzarası* "sea view"), and the noun "friendship".

## Worked answers

1. "Dear Ali, We came to İzmir last Friday. Our hotel is right opposite the sea. Our room is small but spotless. From the window the sea looks deep blue. In the mornings we have breakfast at nine o'clock, and then we go for a long walk on the shore." *Sevgili*: *sevgi* + *-lI* (tur-05-09); *geçen cuma*, no case (tur-05-04).
2. "Everything here is cheaper than in Istanbul, and the weather is warmer too. Yesterday afternoon we sunbathed for two hours. Mehmet swims more than me. He loves both the sea and fish. Next to the hotel there's a small restaurant. There are all kinds of fish, but we liked the sea bass best."
3. "After the meal I bought everyone an ice cream. We're here until 25 August. We've missed you a lot! You neither phone nor send a message. Love, Ayşe."
4. *Karşı-sı-nda*: location noun *karşı* "opposite side" + third-person possessive + pronominal *n* + locative: "at its opposite side" (tur-05-07). *Denizin* is the possessor whose opposite side it is: genitive-possessive (tur-03-05). *Tam* modifies the whole phrase.
5. An emphatic reduplication of *temiz*: *te* + *r* + *temiz* (tur-05-11). Such forms take no degree word (goksel2005 §9.1). *Masmavi* stands right before the verb and describes the sea as it looks: an adjective in the predicate of *görünmek* "look, appear" (tur-05-01).
6. *Sabah-lar-ı*: a time word with *-lArI* for a regular time, "in the mornings" (tur-05-04). *Saat dokuz-da*: clock time on the hour, locative on the number (tur-05-04). *De* links the second clause: "and then" (tur-05-08).
7. *İstanbul-dan*: the ablative as "than" (tur-05-02); the apostrophe before a case on a name (tur-01-08). *Hava da*: "the weather too", *de* after the word it adds (tur-05-08). *Daha* could be dropped, since the ablative already compares: *İstanbul'dan ucuz* (tur-05-02).
8. A length of time takes no case (tur-05-04). *Güneş-len-di-k*: noun "sun", *-lAn* "do to oneself" = sunbathe, past, "we" (tur-05-10, tur-04-03).
9. *Ben-den daha çok*: "more than me": ablative standard, *daha çok* "more" with a verb (tur-05-02). *Hem denizi hem de balığı*: "both ... and", with *de* after the second *hem* for emphasis (tur-05-08). Both objects are things he likes in general: accusative (tur-03-01).
10. *Otel-in yan-ı-nda*: genitive, location noun *yan*, possessive, *n*, locative: "beside the hotel" (tur-05-07). *Çeşit çeşit*: doubled noun, "all kinds of", with *balık* singular (tur-05-11). *En çok*: "most", with a verb (tur-05-02).
11. *Birer* "one each": the receivers (*herkes*) are many, so a distributive fits (tur-05-03). No: after a number word the noun stays singular: *birer dondurma*.
12. *Yirmi beş ağustosa kadar*: the number before the month; in writing the month has a capital and the suffix an apostrophe (tur-05-04). *Kadar* with the dative is "until" (tur-05-06).
13. *Ne ... ne de* makes the sentence negative in meaning even though the verbs are positive: "you neither phone nor send" (tur-05-08; goksel2005 §20.4).
14. "Speech is silver, silence is golden." *Gümüş-se*: noun + conditional copula, "if it is silver" (tur-04-08, preview). *Altın-dır*: noun + *-DIr*, a general statement (tur-04-11).
15. *Ağabeyim benden iki yaş büyük, ama ben daha uzunum.* The amount of difference stands before the adjective; *büyük* "older" with the ablative (tur-05-02). *Uzun-um*: person ending on the predicate (tur-02-02).
16. *Ankara'nın en pahalı üç oteli*, or *Ankara'daki en pahalı üç otel*. With *en*, the number follows the adjective (tur-05-02).
17. *Toplantı ona çeyrek kala başlıyor ve öğleye kadar devam ediyor.* *Kala* after the dative hour (tur-05-04); *kadar* with the dative "until" (tur-05-06); *devam etmek* takes the dative (tur-05-06).
18. *2015'ten beri bu mahallede oturuyorum.* *Beri* with the ablative, verb in the present for a state that continues; *beş* → *-ten* (tur-05-06, tur-05-04).
19. *Evimizin arkasında büyük bir ağaç var, ağacın altında da bir bank var.* Location nouns with the locative (tur-05-07); *de* links the second clause (tur-05-08); *ağacın*: *ç* → *c* before a vowel (tur-01-06).
20. *İşsiz* (*-sIz*), *deniz manzaralı* (*-lI* on the compound, which drops its *-(s)I*, goksel2005 §7.2.2.2), *arkadaşlık* (*-lIK*) (tur-05-09).
21. *Bu üç kitap benim için çok pahalı.* No plural after a number (tur-05-03); *benim için*, genitive pronoun before *için* (tur-05-05); no *-lAr* on a predicate about things (tur-02-03).
22. *Bana göre, Ali Mehmet'ten daha akıllı.* *Göre* takes the dative (tur-05-06); the standard of comparison takes the ablative (tur-05-02).
23. *Toplantıdan beş dakika önce geldim.* The amount stands between the ablative and *önce* (tur-05-06).
24. *Kutu masanın altında*: the pronominal *n* before the case (tur-05-07, tur-03-06). *Ben de bilmiyorum*: the particle *de* is a separate word; *bende* is "on me" (tur-05-08).
25. *Ayşe yepyeni bir elbise aldı*: an emphatic form takes no *çok* (tur-05-11). *Kolayca buldum*: *kolay kolay* only with a negative (tur-05-11); in a positive sentence use *-CA* (tur-05-01).
